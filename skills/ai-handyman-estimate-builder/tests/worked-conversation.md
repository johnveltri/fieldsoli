# Worked conversation · An exterior door with a storm door to keep

**Contractor** — Back entry. Pull the old prehung and set the owner's new one. Keep the storm door. I've done plenty of doors but always end up eating the trim work.

**Builder** — You're replacing the entry unit and handing back a working storm door. Before you price trim, have you compared the new brickmould's mounting face and handle clearance to the storm door? That decides whether you can hang it back as sold. On the inside, what did you promise about casing and paint?

**Contractor** — Good catch. Checked both at the house. New unit works with the storm door. Interior casing is coming off in one piece; reusing it. Owner wants nail holes filled and will paint the room. Opening, jamb depth and threshold height all checked. There's staining at one sill corner. Can't see under it.

**Builder** — I'll include rehanging and checking the storm door, plus resetting the interior casing and filling the nail holes. The owner takes over at paint. At that stained corner, what can you verify now, and where would you stop if the old unit lifts out and the support underneath is soft? Is taking the old unit away yours?

**Contractor** — Exposed surfaces firm. I can't inspect the sill support until the unit's out. If soft, I can do localized sill repair, but I show them and price that first. Need a text yes. Anything into structural framing, I stop and we discuss a carpenter. Yes to haul-away. I'll seal the exterior perimeter, check swing and latch on both. No price yet.
