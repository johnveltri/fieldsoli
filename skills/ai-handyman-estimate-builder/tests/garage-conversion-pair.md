# Garage conversion paired conversation test

Two evaluators independently read the skill and garage conversion interview. Both received the same opening message before seeing their own contractor reply.

“The Garcias want their detached garage finished. My $2,400 is labor to hang and finish wall drywall only. Help me scope it.”

Both asked how the garage would be used, which walls and finish the price covered, who supplied materials and who had work to complete before wall closure.

## A. Existing garage becomes a workshop

**Contractor reply** — Woodworking shop only. All four walls paint-ready. Owner supplies board and mud, clears the garage and paints. Contractor installs backing and fits an eight-foot bench. Overhead door travel checked against bench. Operator, slab and verified tool power stay. Portable dust collection. Ceiling outside scope. Only wall drywall labor priced. Draft now.

**Observed homeowner copy** — “I’ll install the bench backing while the studs are exposed, then hang and finish drywall on all four walls so they’re ready for you to paint. After the wall finish is complete, I’ll fit the eight-foot bench at the agreed location.” The quote put $2,400 on labor to hang and finish those four walls. Bench and backing were unpriced. Contractor review asked who supplies their materials.

**Acceptance** — The quote retained the owner’s painting and material responsibilities and placed backing before drywall. It introduced no residential plumbing or electrical upgrade. Reopening the settled door-clearance question would add noise.

## B. Existing garage becomes an independent ADU

**Contractor reply** — Approved dwelling plan with kitchen and full bath. Contractor frames partitions, insulates to plan, hangs and finishes designated dry-area walls after rough-in release, paints, fits cabinets, doors and trim. Owner supplies board and mud. Separate builder removes garage door and weathers in the entry. Plumber handles water, sewer, sink, toilet and shower connections and testing. Trench path and driveway restoration unsettled. Electrician owns wiring and alarms. HVAC crew handles heat and bath exhaust. Stone shop templates after base installation and mounts sink. Retailer sets appliances. Electric range hookup unassigned. Tile crew waterproofs and tests shower and installs bath tile. Only drywall labor priced. Draft now.

**Observed homeowner copy** — “Once the rough-ins are released for closure, I’ll hang and finish drywall on the designated dry-area walls. You’ll supply the board and joint compound.” The $2,400 covered only that labor. The draft added framing, insulation, paint, cabinets, doors and trim as unpriced contractor work. It assigned the weathertight entry, trade rough-ins, plumbing tests and shower work to their specified crews. The retailer set appliances. Range hookup stayed open.

**Observed contractor review** — Confirm the trench route, excavation/backfill and any driveway restoration before scheduling. Confirm who installs the counter between templating and sink hookup, who connects/tests the range, which dry-area walls are covered and where painting ends. Templating and sink mounting alone did not establish responsibility for installing the top.

| Compare the drafts | Workshop | ADU |
|---|---|---|
| Opening and slab | Retained door and slab, no related installation charge | Builder removes overhead door and weathers in new entry. Trench and driveway responsibilities pending |
| Closure and finish | Bench backing → four paint-ready walls → eight-foot bench. Owner paints | Partitions and insulation, rough-in release, designated dry-area walls, contractor painting and interior fit-out |
| Services and rooms | Existing power and portable collection accepted | Separate electrical, plumbing, HVAC, kitchen fabrication, appliance and bath tile handoffs |
| Price | $2,400 for wall drywall labor only | Same price restricted to dry-area wall drywall labor. Other contractor work unpriced |

Both drafts were marked “Scope draft — pricing to follow.” Neither invented an ADU total, a utility upgrade or a driveway repair fee. The two full paths were conversation-tested; individual variants such as a partly retained vehicle bay were not separately run.
