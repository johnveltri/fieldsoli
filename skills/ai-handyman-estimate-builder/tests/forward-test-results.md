# V1 forward-test results

Two independent evaluators received the skill and contractor prompts. They exercised three cases in chat. The eight conversations in the test suite also provide fixtures for future regression testing.

| Input | Observed first result | Change and observed rerun |
|---|---|---|
| One click plank by a closet, roughly three rows from the wall, same-run spare, $240 bundle including shoe molding, dark line in photo description. Draft now. | Asked whether the jamb trapped the rows and kept the $240 together. Customer copy nevertheless promised to lift and relay flooring before access was confirmed. | Added an instruction to omit or mark unresolved methods in customer copy. Rerun asked about the unlocking direction and jamb, kept the row count approximate, and limited the customer promise to the plank and shoe. |
| Existing pantry pull quote, six pulls for $90, owner supplied, centers verified. Follow-up confirmed fit, full coverage and a request to leave wording alone. | Asked about screw fit and old marks. Proposed documenting the old-pull handoff separately. On the follow-up it returned the contractor's exact quote with no conditions or new charge. | No revision needed for this case. |
| Toilet rocks, rough-in and clearance checked, haul-away and test included. Contractor can repair a flange but does no subfloor work. Draft now. | Asked about visible floor movement and identified a separate framing-of-scope problem: customer copy promised separate pricing for a broken flange before the contractor decided coverage. | Added the distinction between capability and price coverage. Rerun asked whether the base covers flange repair, reserved any price change for confirmation and approval, and stopped at soft or moving subfloor. |

Excerpts from the reruns:

**Plank repair, homeowner draft** — “I’ll replace the damaged plank by the hall closet with one matching plank from your unopened box. The repair includes removing the shoe molding and putting it back in place.” The evaluator kept the supplied total of $240 on the whole repair.

**Toilet replacement, homeowner draft** — “If I find a broken flange, I’ll show you the damage, explain the repair and confirm whether it changes the price. I’ll get your approval for the repair and any price change before proceeding.”

The floor case used a contractor's description of a photo. Image recognition itself was not tested.
