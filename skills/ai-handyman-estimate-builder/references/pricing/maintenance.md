# Residential repair and maintenance price references

U.S. dollars. Provider rates checked November 13, 2026. When a provider gives no effective date, the check date identifies the snapshot. Confirm the same repair, finish, location and visit structure before using any figure as a comparison. These are published service prices or providers' stated planning ranges. They are not paired homeowner payment records.

Finish painting of repaired areas is separately listed at $125. A sticking-door adjustment is $125, interior-door replacement $225, and exterior-door replacement $375. | Urgent handyman call · Evanston, IL | Evanston Handyman lists $125/hour with a one-hour minimum for appointments booked seven or more days ahead, regular-area travel included and materials at actual cost. Record actual crew roles and elapsed hours from the contractor's own similar jobs. The sources establish specific crew rates, visit minimums and regional travel terms. They do not isolate a general apprentice, experienced-handyman or rural productivity multiplier.

**Whole-system HVAC installation.** In Massachusetts' historical residential air-source heat pump program, median reported costs for completed installations in a September 2018–March 2019 holdout were $8,172 with one outdoor unit and $14,200 with two. These are historic project totals before subtracting the recorded rebate. For a present installation, establish indoor head count, outdoor units, served rooms and existing duct or electrical work before seeking a current installer quote. For troubleshooting a system already in place, scope a diagnostic visit separately.
