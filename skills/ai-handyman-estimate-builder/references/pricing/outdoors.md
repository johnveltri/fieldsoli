# Outdoor pricing evidence

U.S. dollars. Sources checked November 13, 2026. Use the contractor's location, measured quantities, selected product and scope to choose comparables. The examples below retain their original market, units and coverage. Verify a current local offer before using a supplier's figures for an order.

## Patios and walkways

- **Pavers alone.** Main's in Southfield, Michigan listed 8 cm Eco-Priora Standard at $5.39/sq ft and Premier at $10.29/sq ft in its 2025 factory-direct sheet. Both ship in full 76-sq-ft pallets. For a 100-sq-ft field with no spares, two pallets supply 152 sq ft before considering the actual cut plan. At list price their respective merchandise totals are $819.28 and $1,564.08 before delivery and tax. The installed field is still 100 sq ft. Confirm current availability and pack-break options. [Main's supplier price sheet](https://mainslandscapesupply.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mains-Landscape-Supply-Southfield-Prices-11-1-25.pdf)
- **Installed work.** Craft Pavers in South Florida lists $12–18/sq ft for concrete, $14–22 for permeable concrete and $22–32 for porcelain, including specified base preparation, pavers, labor, joints, edges and cleanup. Its normal quoting minimum is 300 sq ft. It cites a $1–2/sq-ft addition for deep clay base in its inland market. Use a measured affected area and a confirmed site condition if that work is proposed. This installer prices specialty drainage separately. [Craft Pavers installation table](https://craft-pavers.com/services/paver-installation/)
- **Market and project size.** Install-It-Direct publishes $21–36/sq ft for typical San Diego work with commonly cited areas of 1,000–2,000 sq ft and a $25,000 design-build minimum. A 16-sq-ft rocking-paver reset needs a mobilization, lift, bedding and joint-restoration estimate of its own. Ask about hand-carry distance before selecting the equipment and labor. [Install-It-Direct cost breakdown](https://www.installitdirect.com/pavers-cost/?pp=1)

## Fence and gate

- Measure net fence run when the gate is separately priced. Record height, board layout and support type. Blue Landscaping in South King County, Washington publishes $42–70/linear ft for six-foot cedar privacy and $75–130 for horizontal cedar on steel or 6×6 posts. It shows $550–1,500 per walk gate and $7–14 per foot for removal including footings and haul. Price teardown only over the run actually removed. [Blue Landscaping installed ranges](https://bluelandscapingservices.com/services/landscaping/fencing)
- JB Contracting's 2026 Austin quote-based guide puts six-foot side-by-side cedar at $32–48/ft, board-on-board at $38–55/ft and a four-foot walk gate at $350–750 each. Verify whether old-fence removal is already in the local installed price, whether there is a concrete haul and whether the run can be reached with post equipment. [JB Contracting's Austin guide](https://www.jbcontractingatx.com/blog-cedar-fence-cost-austin.html)

## Deck boards and built decks

- Tacoma supplier Mill Outlet Lumber's April 1, 2026 sheet offers kiln-dried 5/4×6 Alaskan yellow cedar at $2.00 per board linear foot for select tight-knot grade and $2.75 for clear. For 200 purchased linear feet, the material-only difference is $150. Measure actual board coverage and lay out cuts by stock length. Price fastening, framing, access and installation at their own coverage. [Mill Outlet Lumber deck list](https://www.lowpricedcedar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Decking-412026.pdf)
- For a built 16×20-foot deck, Columbus installer Creative Earthscapes advertises starting packages of $23,000 for Edge Prime decking with cedar rails and $26,500 for Terrain decking with different rail choices. Both include fascia, hidden fasteners, three four-foot-wide steps and standard permits. Height over 60 inches may add material. Skirting is separate. The $3,500 spread changes both deck and rail selections. Replacing boards on verified sound framing warrants a separate takeoff. [Creative Earthscapes packages](https://www.creative-earthscapes.com/our-pricing.php)

## Lawn service, sod and mulch

- Price a completed mowing visit against mowable area, frequency and included trim/blow work. Just Right Lawns lists weekly visits at $42–55 up to 5,000 sq ft, $56–81 at 5,001–10,000 sq ft and $84–180 above that in its Texas markets. Small-lawn biweekly rates are $52–65. GG Garden's Sarasota schedule gives $43 per visit under 5,000 sq ft and $50 in its 9,000–10,000 bracket. Its route runs weekly in fast growth and every other week in slower months. Confirm whether the customer measures lawn or whole lot, mower access, overgrowth and clipping handling. Keep the per-visit price when the number of visits is open. [Just Right Lawns](https://justrightlawns.com/about/pricing) · [GG Garden](https://gggardenlawncare.com/price/)
- Bracht Bros in Forest Lake, Minnesota lists natural hardwood mulch at $46/cubic yard and western red cedar at $81/cubic yard, effective April 14, 2026. Delivery has a $30 material minimum plus a quoted delivery charge. Required in-place yards = bed sq ft × added depth in inches ÷ 324. For 216 sq ft at three inches, two cubic yards cost $92 or $162 at list before delivery, tax and spreading. As a separate service example, EasyDay in Minnesota lists spreading at $65/hour for one worker or $120/hour for two. Use measured access and the contractor's actual crew/rate. [Bracht Bros price list](https://brachtbros.com/landscape-supplies-pricing) · [EasyDay service card](https://easydayservicesmn.com/)
- Wagner Sod's Twin Cities store lists pickup sod at $0.65/sq ft. For 90 purchased sq ft the material extension is $58.50 before taxes and any order restrictions. Confirm order units, site prep, trench settlement, water access and who waters after the first day. [Wagner Sod store](https://wagnersod.com/sod-store/)

A site check that resolves a problem changes the base scope immediately. On concealed work, carry the actual discovery and its consequence into a condition. For example, lifting the agreed corner may expose saturated, unstable base. Show the affected footprint, obtain the approved excavation depth or drainage fix, price the revised area, haul and access, then obtain approval before extending the work. A quoted material upgrade and an unknown concealed repair need separate decisions.
