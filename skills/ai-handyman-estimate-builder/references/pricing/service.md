# Small service jobs and appliance installations

Identify the city or ZIP, site readiness, number of visits, number of workers and exactly what the installer hands over. Use the contractor’s supplied prices at their stated coverage. If asked for help setting a price, compare local providers for the same job and explain the scope difference in contractor review. A provider’s first-hour minimum can cover several tasks on one visit. Apply it once. Confirm the contractor’s minimum, included time, additional hourly rate, and how the owner approves extra time.

## U.S. advertised examples, checked November 13, 2026

These are dated comparison points for ordinary residential installations in USD. The midpoint below is the median of sampled provider cards. The endpoints describe those cards only. Check a current local price for the actual work before treating a figure as a bid.

| Service | Sampled basic prices | Details that change the comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Built-in dishwasher | Seven providers, $180–250, median $195. Five with known haul terms, $195–265 including haul, median $235. | Existing opening and services. Match removal, securing, test, supply kit, disconnection, haul and any separate delivery charge. Tucker, GA includes haul at $195 with customer-provided accessories. Voorhees, NJ lists $225 with the plumbing kit and $40 haul. [Tucker installer](https://www.applianceinstallationinc.com/) · [Voorhees retailer](https://www.shopappliancewerks.com/installation.html) |
| Over-range microwave | Five providers, $139–199.95, median $165. Five with known haul terms, $170–259.90 with haul, median $179. | Standard replacement with ready cabinet and existing power. Establish duct mode, bracket/backing, carry, testing and haul. A hood-to-microwave conversion can be a different product. [Grand Appliance](https://www.grandappliance.com/services) · [Carmona’s, Redding CA](https://www.carmonasappliances.com/catalog/add-ons-services) · [Tucker installer](https://www.applianceinstallationinc.com/) |
| Garbage disposer | Six providers, $120–200, median $167. | Existing compatible drain and power, owner-provided unit. Verify flange, reconnects and whether old unit stays. In San Diego a $90 replacement line is subject to a $120 visit minimum. In Middle Georgia a $165 swap assumes suitable connections. [Brown](https://www.handymanbrown.com/rates) · [Pro Appliance Installs](https://proapplianceinstalls.com/services) |
| Standard 55-inch TV | Four initial providers, $120–150, median $132. One Houston card lists $50 extra for drywall over a fireplace and $125 for a fireplace cord cover. Use the actual local package. [Houston mounting card](https://www.themountingman.com/houston/) · [Houston size tiers](https://www.abmtvmounting.com/pricing) · [Smyrna, GA](https://www.alegria.llc/atlanta-handyman-price-list) |
| One-person extra time | Three subsequent-hour rates, $65–94.50/hour. | Smyrna lists $125 covering the first hour and $65/hour thereafter. For shelves, towel bars or other repairs with shared travel, ask whether the contractor prices a visit, a bundle or each installed item. A documented example of projected two-hour cost is useful only when the actual contractor uses that schedule. A $25–100 shelf-hanging range in Central Ohio has unspecified quantity and excludes most materials; it cannot settle a heavy shelf on unverified backing. [Winchester, April 2026](https://winchesterhomeservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/whs_pricelist.pdf)

A cross-provider test on 22 standard project prices put 16 estimates within 20% using the matching service median. On four previously unseen basic rate-card entries, three met that margin. Withheld prices can fall outside the entire observed range. A customer receipt or completed local invoice with the same scope gives more direct evidence of what an owner actually paid.
