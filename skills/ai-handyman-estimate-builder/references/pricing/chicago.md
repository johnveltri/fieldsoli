## When two local installers disagree

Keep the named offers visible. If the customer has not chosen a provider, do not turn one company's fee into **the** local price. For example:

- **Standard 55-inch over-mantel mount, ordinary drywall, customer bracket, visible cable channel:** $207 under the TV Mount Company schedule above. [Media Walls & Smart Homes](https://tvserviceschicago.com/) lists $100 base + $35 external cover; verify that its ordinary bracket/height tier applies over the mantel. [TV Mount Experts, Chicago](https://tv-mount-experts.com/chicago/) lists $199 regular base + $49 fireplace + $69 surface cover = $317; its quiz promotion can lower this to $287. This is a $135 conditional low to $317 regular-price high across these sellers, not one firm range for every fireplace. Do not promise the promotion or recommend a particular mount without the site checks.
- **Pull and reset an existing toilet on a sound flange:** [BootsOnCall, NW suburbs](https://www.bootsoncall.com/plumbing) lists $190–260, typical $225, for pulling, inspecting, supplying a new seal and resetting. Keeley's DuPage card lists $315. The selected contractor's service ZIP and scope decide which comparison applies; neither regional example establishes a Chicago city price.
- **First scheduled handyman hour:** [X-Engineer in Chicago](https://xengineerhandyman.com/) lists $150 first hour, $100/hr thereafter, materials separate. Compare with a first-hour block that includes multiple small tasks; a first-task fee may have a different condition. For an existing $100 contractual first hour, preserve $100 and show market differences only in review.
