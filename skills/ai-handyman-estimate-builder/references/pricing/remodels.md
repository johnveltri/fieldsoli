# Kitchen and bath planning budgets

Ask for the city or ZIP, price year, room size, what stays, what is replaced, quality selections, and who supplies and installs it. For a labor-only subcontract, build the requested trade package from quantities and local costs. A whole-room expenditure includes many parties’ purchases.

## Major remodels with a known room size

When the contractor requests an early budget, give the closest matching row, name the inclusions and year, and label its geography **US national**. A median describes typical reported spending. The 90th percentile shows a high-spend comparison; it is neither an upper cap nor the top of an 80% prediction interval. These are the 2025 expenditures of respondents to the 2026 US Houzz & Home Study, a survey of registered Houzz users. [Houzz study](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/2026-u-s-houzz-and-home-study-renovation-trends-stsetivw-vs~185090855) · [Size and scope breakdown](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/11-things-to-know-about-home-renovations-today-stsetivw-vs~185079173)

| Project and required scope | Room floor area | Median spend | 90th percentile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Kitchen; all cabinets and appliances replaced | Under 200 sq ft | $35,000 | $93,000 |
| Same kitchen scope | 200 sq ft or more | $55,000 | $150,000 |
| Primary bath; vanity/cabinetry, counter and toilet replaced | Under 100 sq ft | $18,000 | $44,000 |
| Same primary bath scope | 100 sq ft or more | $30,000 | $75,000 |

For a 210 sq ft kitchen getting all new cabinets and appliances, an appropriate starting comparison is the $55,000 national median for 2025. Ask about cabinet grade, layout changes, counters and local subcontractor quotes before narrowing it. For a 210 sq ft cabinet-front refresh retaining all boxes and appliances, take off doors, fronts, finish and labor; the major-remodel row describes different work. Avoid interpolation at the group cutoff or converting the values to installation rates.

## Partial remodels with little detail

If the homeowner asks for a rough range while selections are unknown, the 2023 American Housing Survey offers much broader US evidence. A reproducible unweighted analysis of 3,048 training projects in its public project file found these historical bands. They cover projects reported in the preceding two years, in nominal dollars, across size and finish levels. “Outside labor” means the respondent answered no to DIY. The ranges show the 10th and 90th percentiles of that cohort. [Census data and definitions](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/ahs/data/2023.html) · [Home improvement survey questions](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/ahs/2023/2023__AHS_Items_Booklet.pdf)

| Room and work arrangement | Historical middle 80% of training costs | Median |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bathroom, outside labor | $1,800–$30,000 | $9,000 |
| Kitchen, outside labor | $2,500–$60,000 | $13,700 |
| Bathroom, DIY | $450–$10,000 | $2,000 |
| Kitchen, DIY | $600–$21,170 | $4,350 |

Across 790 held-out individual projects, 80% landed in the corresponding bands, but only 22% landed within 25% of the corresponding median. Specify the historical period and cohort; ask what actual tasks the contractor owns. For a current city-specific budget, use comparable completed local jobs, current supplier prices and quantified trade quotes. A prior local-to-national ratio is transferable only with the identical source, same prototype and tier, comparable time period and geography. The tested regional uplift predicts a publisher’s model, and has no demonstrated accuracy for applying a locality factor to these homeowner survey medians.

## When no whole-room comparison fits

For an addition, basement, ADU or mixed whole-house rehab, split the actual project into its nonoverlapping contracted packages: demolition and disposal; exterior shell/structure where applicable; trade rough-ins; preparation/closing; finishes; fixtures; final connection. Price measured quantities at sourced local installed rates, or estimate labor and materials separately. State whether overhead, taxes, allowances, disposal and subcontractor costs are already included. A selected cabinet package or owner-supplied appliance cannot be counted again in a turnkey unit cost. Leave inaccessible, unspecified or unsupported packages visible with their quantities and price status. If every major package lacks a local basis, give the scoping questions and identify the local price input that would let the contractor calculate a budget.

For ADUs, attic and basement conversions, additions, laundry relocation or a whole-house package, consult the [project-specific budgets and scope boundaries](conversions.md).
