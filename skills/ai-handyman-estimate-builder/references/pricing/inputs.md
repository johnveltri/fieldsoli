# Local labor, skill, material and fee inputs

**Labor.** Look up the actual occupation and metro/rural area in [BLS OEWS tables](https://www.bls.gov/oes/tables.htm). General maintenance 49-9071, carpenter 47-2031, painter 47-2141, electrician 47-2111, plumber 47-2152, tile setter 47-2044 and landscaping worker 37-3011 are distinct markets. Use a trade subcontractor's scoped quote for the work they perform. For a crew, price each person's time or use one specified crew rate, not both. Outside a metro use the applicable nonmetro area. Nearby state lines, travel radius, parking and minimum dispatch can matter more than a metro median.

Seven additional trades cover published areas in CA, GA, IL, MA, TX and WA. Find an exact area and occupation before using a row. Missing rows require a fresh OEWS/O*NET lookup. To verify a row, open the O*NET local wage page for its SOC and source state, for example [Illinois general maintenance](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localwages/49-9071.00?st=IL). Use wage vintage and role together.

**Experience and production.** Prefer the contractor's timesheets for comparable installs, crew mix, necessary trips and actual hourly sell rate. Separate helper and lead hours and specify which phases need the lead. Wage percentiles describe distributions, not years-of-experience bands. A faster skilled worker can cost less on the job despite a higher hourly rate. If only a wage is known, identify employer cost/burden, annual overhead, expected billable hours and target margin before computing a sell rate. A simple single-worker planning calculation is (annual compensation and employer costs + allocable annual overhead) / realistic annual billable hours, then apply agreed margin once. Count off-site purchasing, prep, setup, dry-time return and travel in the appropriate cost pool or hours, once. Without the missing inputs report the wage as context and use market service comparables for a budget.

For an observed experience distinction, a Portland OR / Vancouver WA electrical contractor publishes $185/hour for a journeyman and $95/hour for an apprentice on its hourly schedule, plus trip and material terms. Two hours of each would be $560 in worker time; confirm the applicable first-hour/trip arrangement before adding a charge. The same provider has a different short service-call offer. These are one company's role-specific selling rates and imply no general seniority factor. [Energized Electric pricing](https://energizedelectricllc.com/costs/)

For a separate benchmark of retail labor, [Taskrabbit's November 2026 general handyman guide](https://www.taskrabbit.com/cost-guides/general-handyman) blends completed platform jobs and supplemental research. Its listed hourly averages include $60 in Chicago, $65 in the SF Bay Area and $46 in Miami; corresponding average invoice totals are $160, $178 and $119. The service mix and fee coverage vary. Compare the same job type, market and fee basis; do not divide average invoice by average rate to infer an individual job duration.

**Materials and quality.** Capture exact SKU, local branch/ZIP, price date, pack coverage, selected system, availability, freight, tax and purchaser. Compare installed assemblies using correct-grade accessories; an owner-supplied appliance still may require model-specific kits. Order quantity = ceiling(required coverage including justified layout waste / sale-unit coverage). Billable installed quantity can differ. Use low/high based on actual selectable products. Do not use a blanket premium percentage or substitute a different performance specification.

A US retail listing checked Nov 13, 2026 showed 4 × 8 ft sheets at $16.68 for standard ½-inch USG 14113411708 and $23.58 for ½-inch Mold Tough 14302111708. At 34+ sheets the listed unit prices were $14.18 and $20.04 respectively. The selected grade changes the 10-sheet material subtotal from $166.80 to $235.80 before tax, delivery, compound, fasteners and labor. The displayed store market was unspecified. Refresh at the project store; specialty board is selected for suitability, not merely as a price tier. [Supplier listing](https://www.homedepot.com/b/Building-Materials-Drywall/USG-Sheetrock-Brand/Drywall/Bulk-Pricing/N-5yc1vZar3dZomdZbwo6kZ1z10nw4)

For coating work, obtain the actual can size, line, sheen and substrate coverage per coat. Multiply net wall area by coat count and divide by specified coverage, then order whole cans. A supplier's product-family range may include both quarts and gallons. Use the [specific SKU and size](https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/product/regal-select-waterborne-interior-paint-eggshell-1-gallon/N549) for a local quote.

**Site complications.** Quantify a narrow carry route by extra person-trips, a two-person lift by both workers' hours, occupied work by setup per phase and return visits, a failed substrate by measured repair units after exposure. Confirm cut and join patterns, stairs, heights, access equipment, staging, haul distance, dump minimum, access permissions and permit fees only when relevant. For Seattle loads as of April 1, 2026 the transfer-station fee for garbage was $174/ton with a $37 trip minimum; sorted clean yard/wood waste $132/ton with a $25 minimum. A half-ton garbage load costs $87 at that facility, before trucking and labor. Price mixed loads by the actual facility rules. [Seattle Public Utilities](https://www.seattle.gov/utilities/your-services/collection-and-disposal/transfer-stations/rates)

For permit or trade review costs use the job jurisdiction's calculator, valuation, trade and fee date. [Portland's permit estimate service](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/building-permit-estimates) distinguishes building, review, trade and system charges. Use an allowance only when its coverage and reconciliation are clear. For uncertain concealed conditions agree on how findings and a separate price will be approved before extra work.
