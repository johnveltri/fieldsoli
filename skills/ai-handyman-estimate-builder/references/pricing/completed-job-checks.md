# Check the components that move the price

Use these checks for historical comparisons and current planning. All numbers are nominal USD. Ask who bought each part, what was installed, which operations shared a visit, and whether the contractor sold time, an installed package or a product with installation as an add-on. For homeowner spending, show owner purchases plus contractor payments separately; for a contractor estimate exclude owner purchases from the price due. A material receipt included with a contractor invoice is not a second purchase.

**Paint and patch.** Sketch wall area as perimeter × height less major openings; measure ceilings and linear trim separately. Identify the number of coats on each surface. Quote application, protection, cut-in, localized crack repairs vs full skim/sand, cleanup and any second mobilization. A deadline alone does not establish a rush fee. When the homeowner omits areas or crack lengths, show a light-preparation base and a measured repair alternative; do not budget extensive structural patching or a full skim by default. For a whole house ask for paintable facade area, height/access, prep, coating and color transitions before using a house-size allowance. Interior rolling is not an exterior or a plaster production rate. A historical cross-check: [Craftsman, 2013 National Painting Cost Estimator, p. 7](https://www.craftsman-book.com/media/static/previews/2013_NPC_book_preview.pdf) gives 538 ft² per painter-hour and 275 ft²/gallon in its medium example for the first coat rolled on textured drywall. At 1,000 ft² that is 1.86 application hours and 3.64 gallons before rounding, not a price for a finished room. Its material example was $32.80/gallon. Estimate the remaining operations separately; shared setup belongs once. Do not apply 2013 dollars to a current job.

**Specialist installed prices.** For a heat-pump changeout, identify the matched indoor/outdoor units, tonnage, stages, acquisition channel, reuse of lines/ductwork, removal and commissioning. An observed crew's elapsed time measures production; it does not establish the contracted installation price. Check the complete install package before using time and materials. The [DOE historical technical support document, table 8.2.30](https://downloads.regulations.gov/EPA-HQ-OAR-2021-0643-0228/attachment_28.pdf) gives a national 2-ton split heat-pump installation model of $920 single-speed or $979 two-speed using 2015 cost data, **excluding equipment**. Its accounting recovers some costs through equipment markups. A deal at wholesale cannot assume that recovery; verify the installation fee, procurement, commissioning, warranty obligations and what the price includes. Do not invent a markup or add overhead twice.

For equipment service, match the specific installed part and warranty, not a generic part at retail. [Upfront AC's Greater Houston menu](https://www.upfrontac.com/upfront-pricing), checked Nov 2026, lists a standard dual 30–60 MFD capacitor at $259, Turbo 200 at $361, and 5-2-1 kits at $259/$289/$319 depending on tonnage. These are one firm's repair prices; check whether diagnosis or maintenance is included, waived or a separate line. A multi-unit visit does not necessarily get multiple trip charges. As an illustration of provider variation, [Frosty's Fort Worth menu](https://www.frostyshvac.com/ac-repair-fort-worth-tx) lists $650 for a hard-start kit with a separate membership price. Neither menu is a dated offer for another firm or a 2022 invoice. For retrospective work prefer a year-matched source; label any later check explicitly.

**Springs and hardware.** Record door size, number and type of springs, cycle rating, warranty, urgency and whether the price includes labor. A [Maryland installer’s April 2026 menu](https://eaglegaragedoorservices.com/blog/garage-door-spring-replacement-cost-maryland/) lists standard installed torsion pairs at $300–550, high-cycle pairs at $400–700 and an emergency increment of $50–100. This is one provider, not a maximum. For multi-part work, get the same-visit incremental or full overhaul price; a lifetime service contract is not the same as a parts warranty. [Mainspring's Houston market guide](https://mainspringgaragedoortexas.com/blog/garage-door-repair-pricing-breakdown-what-houston-homeowners-pay-in-2026/) puts a stand-alone cable pair at $160–280 and a full roller set at $140–260 installed (2026). Avoid summing multiple complete service calls or pricing premium assemblies as loose hardware. Refer tensioned-door work to a door specialist.

**Locks and supplied fixtures.** Count the hardware pieces, match grade, finish, keying and whether the openings are already bored. Separate hardware, installation and any actual trip fee; a contractor’s product price can differ from a retail two-pack. The [Locksmith Ledger late-2016 US subscriber survey](https://www.locksmithledger.com/home/article/12286536/2017-national-average-price-survey-results) reported $74 for a regular call, $92 after hours, $60 labor for a cylindrical deadbolt and $65 for a cylindrical lever. These are independent survey categories, not mandatory additive fees; a lever is not a knob or historic door restoration. With owner-supplied appliances and fixtures, check the product receipt separately from standard installation, hookup kit, haul-away, and tax. For water heaters distinguish the heater from vent/pipe alterations, connector kit, permits and disposal. The [2011 Southern California plumbing supplier sheet](https://ocapsupply.com/2010/PriceBook2011PriceB.pdf) priced a comparable American Standard 50-gallon gas unit at $452.85 and a gas install kit at $34.29. Neither includes installation or proves an exact competing SKU's price.

**Mixed handyman visits.** Separate the time and stock materials for each task, then apply a single trip/minimum if actually charged. Houston provider submissions in [ProMatcher's local report](https://handyman.promatcher.com/cost/houston-tx-handyman-costs-prices.aspx) ranged from $25–45/hour plus materials (Prime Solutionz, 2020; generally quoted per project) to $75–200/hour plus materials (Sanchez-Molina, 2022). Ask which arrangement applies, and whether a trip minimum or a return is needed. These observations cannot identify one firm's billed hours or a specialist's fixed price.

**Size the add-on before assigning hours.** For a second task during a booked visit, first ask what can be finished on site with tools already out. For a high spot of soil, get length × width × mean depth (ft³; divide by 27 for yd³), onsite destination, carry distance and any disposal. A few shovel loads moved on site and excavation with loading and haul-away are different jobs. If quantity is missing, quote the known primary work and give the add-on as incremental time × the already-agreed rate plus documented supplies. Do not automatically assign half a day, a second minimum, hauling or equipment. Apply this same test to extra hardware, caulk, touch-ups and other punch-list additions.

**Historic masonry and unusual warranties: when to stop at a subtotal.** A phrase such as “line with refractory coating” does not establish a new full-length liner on each chimney. Ask which flue(s), measured length, repair area, whether work is spot repair, resurfacing or a new liner, and what shared staging is needed. [HeatShield, the manufacturer](https://heatshieldchimney.com/chimney-liner-repair-systems/) distinguishes joint repair, resurfacing and relining. Obtain the specialist's measured bid for the requested method; no area or method means no single total from a per-chimney guess. Price documented cleaning separately only where it is not bundled. [Chimcare, Spokane](https://www.chimcare.com/location/chimney-concrete-repair-in-spokane-wa/) lists $299 for sweep with inspection and quotes repairs after inspection (checked November 2026); that price does not buy a masonry repair or a historic job in an earlier year. Likewise, a door overhaul sold with a lifetime warranty needs the dealer's actual package terms, coverage for labor/parts and an installed quote. If only standard-spring pricing is available, show that comparison by name and leave the premium package pending, rather than issuing an assumed premium price.

**Interior finish and exterior restoration.** A full skim of walls *and ceiling* is a separate operation from local crack repair or paint prep. Price returns for drying and sanding, access, finish standard and room surface area. For an unmeasured two-level historic exterior ask for elevation photos/dimensions and scraping, repair and protection requirements. When these control most of the cost, give the measured parts or a conditional quantity example and withhold a homeowner-ready total until measurements or a trade price are available. A useful subtotal is preferable to a very wide range built on an invented building size.
