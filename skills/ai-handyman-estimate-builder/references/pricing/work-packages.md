# What to measure and where to price it

Use this alongside the [task directory](../scenarios/tasks.md). For each **covered** line cite the actual adopted source. This directory is a costing route and does not assign universal unit prices. Where only assembly benchmarks exist, do not break them into guessed line prices. An unusual or specialist item may still have a useful priced assessment even when repair needs a site visit.

| Task families | Billable basis and quantities | Evidence and important cost boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Scope and scheduling; handoff | Site/measurement fee, coordination hours, trips, tests | Contractor terms or local service card. Keep actual design, inspection, mobilization and return appointments at their specified coverage. |
| Protection and removal; moving and haul away; specialty handling | Crew-hours, floor protection area, item count, truck loads/weight, travel and facility minimum | Local mover, specialty mover, equipment rental and facility ticket. Narrow stair, occupied carry, salvage, curb staging and licensed handling change what the truck price buys. |
| Material assessment; water intrusion and recovery; pest and wildlife repairs | Inspection/test fee, defined remediation and separate reinstatement | Use assessor, mitigation contractor or pest service quote. No implied remediation from the symptom or per-sq-ft paint rate. |
| Groundwork; foundations and structure | Excavation volume/access, mobilization, compacted base, footing count, concrete, reinforcement, inspections | Local aggregate/concrete yard, short-load and pump charges, specialist bids and engineer. Use paid-project totals only for scope comparisons. |
| Walls and ceilings; painting and coatings; cleaning and seasonal service | Patch count/area, finish level, wall area × coats, visits, service-event frequency | [Maintenance prices](maintenance.md), [material examples](inputs.md), local painter or cleaner. Charge travel minimum once when shared. High ceiling, cure and color change affect hours and visits. |
| Flooring; tile and wet assemblies | Installed area, layout-specific purchased cartons, demo, subfloor, transitions, waterproofing, shower floor vs wall | Local stock/grade, box coverage, substrate release, labor or specialty proposal. No extension of field rate to stairs, niches or tiny repairs without coverage. |
| Cabinets and counters; trim and millwork; furniture repair | Cabinets/front count, linear feet/profile, slab template and cutouts, finish passes | Cabinet shop, fabricator, matching part and carpenter. Retained boxes, owner-supplied slab, one-off restoration and stock trim each use different inputs. |
| Mounting and backing; assembly and anchoring; access and safety fittings | Items, anchoring locations, load/backing, crew-hours/minimum | SKU-specific assembly offer or [service](service.md); separate wall openings, patch, cord management and fixtures. Distinguish supply and labor. |
| Doors and locks; windows and screens; exterior envelope | Openings, slab vs prehung, sash vs glass, trim and water-management interfaces | Actual unit/lock/mesh and matching installer/glazier quote. Weathered tie-ins, backing and finish coverage change labor. |
| Drainage and gutters; insulation and air sealing | Linear run, access height, verified discharge, thickness/coverage and air sealing | Local material/installer. Include access, old insulation and testing only where agreed. |
| Decks stairs and rails; fences and gates; masonry and paving | Net installed footage or area; plus footings, stairs, rails, gates, preparation | [Outdoor rates](outdoors.md). Confirm grade, sections, opening deduction, finish and salvage. Scope footing and drainage for the actual soil. |
| Yard and irrigation; outdoor equipment | Per completed visit or measured turf/bed area, quantities, plant sizes, irrigation zones, kit and base | [Outdoor rates](outdoors.md), nursery, installer, local waste facility. Plants/haul on installation day and recurring service have distinct units. |
| Water supply and fixtures; waste and drains | Fixture/connection, existing compatible swap vs new rough-in, access, shutoff, leak/operation test | [Service rates](service.md) or local plumber quote. Survey fixture spend may include hardware. |
| Power and controls; ventilation and comfort; fuel and combustion | Model, capacity, circuit/duct/pipe run, design, permits and commissioning | Local qualified trade quote. For installed air-source heat pumps compare indoor and outdoor unit counts using the [Massachusetts program evidence](maintenance.md). EPA's published US heat-pump water-heater context gives $1,500–$3,000 equipment plus $1,000–$3,000 installation labor/materials. Confirm equipment, scope and local sell price, and keep incentives separate. [ENERGY STAR source](https://www.energystar.gov/homesavings/heat-pump-water-heaters/learn?zipcode=97701) |
| Technology and alarms; garage doors and operators | Device/model, diagnosis, install, pairing/commissioning, access/cabling or specialist appointment | Local specialist assessment, SKU and installation offer. Network troubleshooting, torsion repair and a homeowner account handoff have different boundaries. |
| Appliance installation; appliance diagnosis | Per model and hookup, service call/diagnosis, labor vs supply, kits, first vs additional units | Use the [20 appliance branches](../scenarios/appliances.md) and [service cards](service.md). Fit, water/drain, vent, power/fuel, cabinets, stairs, haul and return to test can change the scope. Verify whether diagnostic credit applies to repair. |
| Pool spa and pond; private water systems | Visit, diagnosis, quoted equipment, specialist installation, water testing | Local pool/well/septic specialist quote, equipment model and access. AHS septic category is historical project-level spending only. |

For a project containing multiple rooms or phases, use the [27 project maps](../scenarios/projects.md) and price each contracted package above. Check [remodel comparisons](remodels.md) and [conversion and addition evidence](conversions.md) only when the described scope matches. A $/sq-ft whole-house number cannot price a carpenter's labor-only portion. For a [homeowner symptom](../scenarios/calls.md), price the authorized diagnosis before the repair cause is verified. For [marketplace bookings](../scenarios/marketplace.md), check contract minimums, timing, exchange/return, disposal and platform fees for the specific offer.

## Specialist comparisons to check against the local handoff

- **Pool maintenance, Brevard County FL.** A provider lists weekly residential service starting at $145/month, every-other-week service from $105/month and one-time cleaning from $150. Size, existing condition and visit contents matter. Use the provider's stated monthly or per-job unit. Checked Nov 13, 2026. [Orbit Pools](https://orbitpools.com/pricing)
- **Private well, southern California.** A local well contractor describes pressure switch replacement at $150–$350 and control box swaps at $250–$600. A deep submersible pull needs a separate specialist scope. A no-water symptom does not establish which of these jobs applies. Checked Nov 13, 2026. [Southern California Well Service](https://scwellservice.com/cost-calculator.html)
- **Wildlife and finish repairs.** A 2024 Cleveland-area wildlife provider guide priced a squirrel inspection needing a ladder or interior access at $90, with per-animal removal starting at $100. Confirm current local scope, access, visits and specialist price. Estimate the handyman's repair of the entry point after release as a separate package. [Lakewood Exterminating 2024 guide](https://www.lakewoodexterminating.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024-Price-Guide-Lakewood-Exterminating.pdf)
