# Fixtures and owner purchases

## Faucet or vanity replacement

“Does the actual faucet fit the sink holes and clear the backsplash through the handle's full travel?” Verified fit → include fitting and agreed leak/operation check. Known mismatch → agree an alternate faucet or compatible plate before ordering. For a vanity, ask where the drain and supplies land relative to the new drawers. Clear → scope in-place reconnection. Drawer interference → settle a different cabinet or agreed plumbing modification within capability before quoting.

“What will the narrower vanity expose at the floor and wall?” Finished surfaces verified → no restoration line. Missing tile or unpainted strip visible or measurable now → settle coverage, patch or finish boundary. Overlooked case: a supplied pop-up drain may require an overflow-compatible basin. Verify the pair before promising installation. If accessible stops already fail to isolate, agree shutdown responsibility and timing in advance. Skip asking the brand of the old faucet when confirmed dimensions and connections settle fit.

## Toilet replacement

“Have you verified the rough-in from the finished wall and the tank clearance for this model?” Confirmed → include setting that toilet, reconnecting and testing. Known mismatch → owner exchanges or selects another fixture before scheduling. “Does the base rock, and what can you actually see of the floor and flange?” Known uneven finish → settle how a stable set will be delivered; concealed broken flange → show it after lifting and obtain a repair price approval before proceeding. Soft subfloor outside the contractor's scope → stop and discuss who assesses it.

Overlooked case: a smaller new footprint can reveal an unfinished flooring cut. Compare footprints while the models can still be changed; settle the visible finish expectation. Skip questions about opening the ceiling below a straightforward swap without evidence of damage or a need for access.

## Customer-supplied fixture or hardware

“Which pieces are in the box, and what existing dimensions or fittings have you checked them against?” Verified complete and compatible → scope labor for the specified item. Missing adapter or wrong model → assign purchase or exchange and a due date before dispatch. If sealed until the owner arrives, identify that as a readiness check; only include return-trip pricing if the contractor actually supplies and agrees its terms.

“Does your promise include making the old footprint disappear?” Comparable coverage → include the confirmed swap and operation check. Smaller plate, handle or canopy → agree on visible marks, an alternate part or defined restoration. Overlooked case: a keyed entry set can fit the bore but fail the owner's same-key handoff. If shared keying is mentioned, confirm who arranges matching cylinders before install. Skip shopping preferences when the exact part, fit and finish have been accepted.
