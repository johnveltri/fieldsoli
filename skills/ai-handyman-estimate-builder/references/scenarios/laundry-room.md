# Laundry room interview

“Where do the machines work today, where will they run when you leave, and what is the owner expecting to do with the old space?” A pullout for flooring, a carry to another room and an installation on new services involve different work. Identify the actual models, who owns each connection, and which surfaces and visits the contractor priced. ## Refresh in the existing room

| Clue | Ask on the walk | Effect on scope |
|---|---|---|
| Floor replacement under a working pair | Does the floor run beneath both machines? Where can they stand while the surface is prepared and ready for loading, and who disconnects and returns them? | Whole floor → include agreed pullout, protected staging and return after the specified floor is ready. Separate trade does hookups → name their visit. Floor stops at machines → confirm the agreed visible boundary and check future extraction past the lip. ## Relocate to a new room

1. **Measure the move and the installed position.** Check doors, turns, stairs, final depth with connected hoses/duct, opening of lids and doors, and later removal. Who supplies model-specific transport hardware or stacking kit if needed? A ready side-by-side pair can move as agreed. A closet conversion may require a door change, lift assistance or an owner decision on models. For an upstairs move, settle support, vibration concerns, any required leak protection and the destination for a pan drain before closing the floor. [Moving](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Stacked pair](laundry.md#stacked-laundry-or-laundry-center)
2. **Follow the washer services all the way.** Where can the plumber isolate hot/cold supplies and route the machine discharge, trap and vent? A sink on the opposite wall is a clue to investigate. Confirm actual capacity, elevation, access and the receiving drain. Verified route → assign connection box, accessible stops and rough/final work. Unknown path through the occupied room below → keep route approval, opening, closing and painting responsibility visible before quoting that restoration. [Supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Waste](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Washer](laundry.md#clothes-washer)
3. **Choose the dryer branch by model.** For a vented unit, can the proposed duct route and fittings meet the model requirements and reach an agreed exterior termination? Assign the penetration, weather sealing and verification. If the duct crosses a finished room outside the quoted paint area, settle that opening and restoration with the owner before cutting. Confirm fuel or electrical supply and connection owner. For a heat-pump or other ventless model, verify tank access or condensate discharge and power. For a combo, include the wash connections too. Remove the proposed new exterior duct if the chosen unit uses none. [Vented dryer](laundry.md#vented-dryer) · [Ventless dryer or combo](laundry.md#ventless-dryer-or-all-in-one) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Fuel](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion)
4. **Set the finish and trade sequence.** Who arranges any applicable permit and releases rough work before wall closure? Identify cabinet blocking, wall patch/paint boundaries, flooring preparation, baseboards and thresholds. Ask whether the existing laundry can stay operational until the destination is ready. Agree downtime, protected staging and any return only when required. A quoted floor area belongs to its specified room. [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing) · [Walls](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings) · [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork)
5. **Decide what the old room becomes.** Are the old hookups to remain usable for a future pair or be retired? Retained → agree who disconnects, secures and leaves access, and what the owner should see. Retired → assign the applicable water, drain, electrical, gas and exhaust closure to named trades, including who closes the former exterior opening and restores each disturbed finish. If the old room's future use is undecided, ask before including permanent closure or a full repaint. 6. **Assign the first working cycle.** Does the contractor leave leveled machines in position or both connected and tested? Name the owners for the washer fill/drain/leak check and the dryer's agreed operation and exhaust or condensate check. Verify access to stops and maintenance points with doors and cabinetry installed. Assign shared moving or testing once.
