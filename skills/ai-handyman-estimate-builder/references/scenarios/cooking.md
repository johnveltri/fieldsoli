# Cooking and exhaust appliances

Open the entry for the actual purchase. Confirm model instructions and who owns the linked tasks before adding them to an estimate.

## Electric or induction range

Does the selected model match the verified electrical supply, anti-tip attachment and recess for the cord at final depth?

Compatible: scope the agreed cord/setup, placement, leveling, anti-tip engagement and function check. Mismatched supply: schedule the responsible electrician before installation; keep the range price attached to its original scope.

A raised floor or projecting receptacle can keep a slide-in range proud of the counter. Check final position. Skip cookware questions for an installation-only booking.

Related tasks include [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring).

## Gas or dual-fuel range

Who is making and testing the gas connection, and does this particular range also need a different electric supply?

Confirmed installer and suitable services: assign placement, securing, connection and test to their agreed owners. Fuel mismatch or missing electric service: settle approved model/conversion and trade visit before promising startup.

A dual-fuel range can match the old gas connection while the oven supply differs. Skip conversion-kit discussions once fuel and configuration are confirmed.

Related tasks include [Fuel and combustion](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff).

## Cooktop or downdraft unit

Do the actual cutout and clearances below fit the appliance body and the downdraft path with the drawers in place?

Match: scope fitting and agreed hookup/test. Too small: assign counter modification and dust protection before setting. Too large: settle supported fit or revised top with fabricator before removal.

A matching top width can hide a deeper blower that collides with a drawer. Skip duct questions for a cooktop served by a separate unchanged hood.

Related tasks include [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Fuel and combustion](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion).

## Wall oven, double oven or microwave drawer

Who verified the shelf support, cutout depth and lift route for this exact unit?

Ready cabinet: include agreed lifting, setting, securing, aligning and testing. Unsupported base or oversized cutout: assign cabinet preparation and trim fit before scheduling the lift.

For a drawer, check full extension past an island pull; for double ovens, check handling access before promising a solo visit. Skip cooktop cutout measurements on a standalone oven column.

Related tasks include [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Moving and haul away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls).

## Over-the-range microwave

Does the new bracket/template suit the cabinet, and does the exhaust outlet line up with the selected vent mode?

Aligned duct and mount: include bracket, mounting, connection and fan/light check. Recirculating design selected: confirm kit and discharge clearance. Misaligned external duct: settle reroute and cabinet repair before offering that result.

Deep side trim can block the hinge even when the opening width matches. Skip exterior termination questions once recirculation is chosen and verified.

Related tasks include [Mounting and backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls).

## Range hood or insert

Is the homeowner asking for filtered recirculation or exhaust outdoors, and who owns the liner/cabinet opening?

Compatible existing duct: fit hood and verify agreed fan operation. New outdoor route: identify who creates, seals and finishes the penetration. Recirculation: assign compatible filters and clear outlet instead.

An insert may arrive without its required liner or blower. Check the purchased components before making the cabinet cut. Skip filter-purchase questions that apply only to another configuration.
