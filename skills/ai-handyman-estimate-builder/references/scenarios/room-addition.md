# Room addition interview

“What should the owner be able to walk into from the existing house, and what will already be built when your crew starts?” Mark the new footprint, the opening into the house, each room and the last existing surface the contractor will restore. A finish installer may only need to know who hands over a dry, inspected shell. For a contractor responsible for the whole addition, follow the interfaces below. [Parent project](projects.md#room-addition-or-second-story) · [Scope and scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling)

## From site layout to an enclosed bedroom

| Decision | Ask on the walk | Change to scope |
|---|---|---|
| Site and design | Ground-level extension or second story? Who verifies layout, approvals, existing structure and service locations? Where will equipment enter and roof runoff go? | For a ground-level build, assign excavation, footings/foundation, grading and affected paving or yard. For a second story, follow the accepted load path, roof removal, temporary cover and stair access. A finish-only contractor records shell readiness as a prerequisite. [Groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters) · [Stairs](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) |
| House opening | Is this an existing door being reused or a new opening through a load-bearing exterior wall? Can the house stay enclosed until the addition is dry? | Existing approved opening → define threshold and casing. New opening → assign design, temporary support, framing, any service relocation and weather/dust protection. Identify who restores the *existing* room, including how far paint or flooring must blend. [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Paint](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) |
| Roof, wall and floor junctions | Who owns flashing where the new roof meets the old wall, siding reinstatement and the floor height at the passage? | Approved connection and compatible finish heights → include agreed dry-in and transition. Different roof pitch, siding profile or floor height → settle the detail and finish owner before quoting. Damage concealed at the junction needs an observable trigger and an agreed repair/price approval step if actually outside base coverage. [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Insulation](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) |
| Bedroom use | Who confirms the sleeping-room escape opening on the local plan? Will the room heat and cool with the door shut, and who supplies lights, outlets and applicable alarms? | Verified plan and assigned trades → record window placement and trim, mechanical/electrical handoffs, and release before wall closure. Unconfirmed system capacity or opening design → flag for verification before promising occupancy. Skip plumbing routes when the addition is a dry bedroom and no existing services are displaced. Which walls, ceilings, doors, baseboards and closets are included? | Measure each agreed surface and keep quantities attached to the room. ## Primary suite with a bathroom and walk-in closet

Build on the bedroom and shell decisions above. Count common foundation, roof, mobilization and house-opening work once. Check a figure quoted for the bedroom against the actual surfaces in the bath and closet before extending it.

1. **Place the wet wall before closing the foundation or floor.** Where do toilet, shower and vanity connect to verified supplies, hot water, waste and vent? Same-wall connection with accessible routes → assign rough and final plumbing. A drain crossing a beam or finished room below → settle routing, structural detail, access and who restores the existing finishes. Who must check the below-floor work before the slab is poured or subfloor closed? [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Closure](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings)
2. **Set bath finish and trade handoffs.** Confirm the selected tub or shower, threshold and any backing for bars, glass or seating. Who connects the drain, owns the waterproofing at the junction and verifies it before tile? Identify the exhaust route, exterior termination, lighting, controls and owner of each penetration. Assign tile surfaces, vanity, fixtures, glass measurement/return after tile and final tests. For an accessible layout, confirm the resident’s transfer side and doorway clearance before framing. [Bathroom interview](bathroom.md) · [Fixtures](fixtures.md) · [Wet assemblies](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies) · [Accessibility](tasks.md#access-and-safety-fittings) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort)
3. **Define what makes the closet complete.** Painted walk-in with shelf and rod, owner-supplied modular kit, or fitted cabinets with drawers? For a shell-only handoff, define walls, lighting, door, floor and base and name the installer taking over. For a fitted interior, confirm supplier, measured wall runs, rod/shelf layout, backing and final installation visit. A usable aisle depends on hanging clothes and extended drawers, not just wall-to-wall width. Check door swing, switch access and whether baseboards obstruct a floor-standing system. Confirm backing before drywall and finished dimensions before ordering custom pieces. [Closet fit-out](projects.md#garage-closet-or-home-office-fit-out) · [Cabinetry](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters) · [Backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls)
4. **Walk the bedroom-to-bath and bedroom-to-closet thresholds.** Confirm floor build-up, door undercuts and where each installer stops. A flooring price limited to bedroom area stays there even if matching plank continues into the closet. Identify the installer and pricing status of that extension; keep bathroom tile under the bath scope. Record who finishes the entry from the existing house, tests fixtures and fans, fits the closet interior and hands back the suite.
