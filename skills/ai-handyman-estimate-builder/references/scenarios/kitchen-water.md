# Kitchen appliances with water or refrigeration

Open the entry for the actual purchase. Confirm model instructions and who owns the linked tasks before adding them to an estimate.

## Built-in dishwasher

New flooring, stone counter or tight opening. Can the old unit clear the finished floor and can the new unit attach at the sides or top as its manual allows?

Clear path and compatible mount: remove the old unit if agreed, secure the replacement, connect and run the agreed fill/drain/leak check. A verified trapped unit: settle who lifts trim or restores flooring before promising a swap.

Resolving extraction settles only removal. If mounting under the new counter is still unverified, keep it in contractor review before sending the swap estimate.

Does the sink drain go through a newly fitted disposer? Assign a single person to verify the dishwasher drain path and test both together. Skip a whole-kitchen floor measurement once extraction is confirmed.

## Portable or drawer dishwasher

Is this a rollaway unit used at the sink or a drawer fitted behind a cabinet front?

Rollaway: confirm tap adapter, hose reach and storage position; scope setup and demonstration. Drawer: confirm cutout, panel supplier and attachment points; include alignment and a drain check for each installed drawer.

A pull-out spray faucet may change adapter compatibility; check before promising the portable unit will connect. Skip cabinet alterations for a rollaway.

Related tasks include [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters).

## Refrigerator or freezer

With the hinge against that return wall, can the door open far enough to remove the drawers? Does the purchase include an ice or water hookup?

Measured clearance and existing compatible supply: scope placement, leveling, connection and leak check as agreed. Blocked swing: settle alternative position, permitted hinge change or model selection before delivery.

An appliance may fit the niche yet fail the turn on the stairs. Confirm access with packaging/doors as applicable. For a dry freezer skip supply and filter questions.

Related tasks include [Moving and haul away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures).

## Panel-ready refrigerator, wine or beverage cooler

Does the selected model accept the ordered panel and work with the proposed toe-kick ventilation opening?

Cabinetmaker delivers finished panels: include agreed mounting, reveal adjustment and door test. Panels unavailable: owner or cabinetmaker confirms delivery; identify whether setting now means a return for panels. Freestanding model in an enclosed bay: resolve suitability first.

A continuous plinth can block the appliance grille even when the cabinet width is right. Skip custom-panel detailing for a factory-finished front.

Related tasks include [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Trim and millwork](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort).

## Standalone ice maker

Where is the drain relative to the outlet, and which drainage arrangement does this model support?

Confirmed gravity route: scope the agreed supply, drain connection and discharge check. Route needs lift: confirm compatible pump, who supplies it and drain destination before quoting completion.

Check that the service pullout leaves connections accessible. Skip promising ice production during a short visit unless the model and agreed test time support it.

## Garbage disposer or instant hot dispenser

Ask whether the existing sink flange stays. If the contractor confirms a matching flange swap, name removal and replacement of the flange alongside the disposer in the customer line.

For a disposer, where does its discharge sit relative to the trap and dishwasher branch? For a hot dispenser, is there a compatible tap hole and space for the tank and its service connections?

Like-for-like disposer geometry: scope mount, drain hookup and leak/run test; changed height: settle trap rework before quoting. Dispenser with prepared hole and utilities: fit tank and tap; missing hole in stone: owner coordinates fabricator or selects another arrangement.

Deep sink plus deeper disposer may put the discharge too low for the existing inlet. Verify actual elevations. Skip dishwasher questions on a dispenser-only job.
