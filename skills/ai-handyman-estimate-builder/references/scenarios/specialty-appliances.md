# Comfort, water treatment and other appliances

Open the entry for the actual purchase. Confirm model instructions and who owns the linked tasks before adding them to an estimate.

## Window or portable AC

Which window opens, how does it move, and is the appliance supported in the opening or standing on the floor?

Compatible window unit: scope the specified support, seal and agreed removal/storage. Portable unit: fit its exhaust panel and establish condensate handling. Incompatible opening: settle accessory or alternate location before booking.

A casement crank can obstruct an otherwise correctly sized panel. Skip permanent refrigerant-line work for a self-contained appliance.

Related tasks include [Windows and screens](tasks.md#windows-and-screens), [Mounting and backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls).

## Water heater, softener or filter

Is it cartridge service, a whole treatment unit, or a new heater? Who handles bypass/drain for treatment, or fuel, vent and startup for the heater?

Cartridge only: specify housing, correct consumable and leak check. Softener: confirm bypass, discharge and regeneration handoff. Heater: allocate removal, fuel/power, water connections, model-specific vent/drain work and commissioning; changed service stays open until assigned.

Can the filter bowl drop far enough for the next cartridge change? For a heater tank, verify carry access and agreed haul-away. Skip burner questions on a filter service call.

Related tasks include [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Fuel and combustion](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort).

## Outdoor grill, pizza oven or patio heater

Is this a portable kit or a built-in with permanent fuel, support and specified enclosure clearances?

Portable: assemble, place and demonstrate the agreed functions. Built-in: confirm approved base, enclosure, fuel supply and who tests it before setting. Wood-fired model: establish support, clearances and agreed handoff for that unit.

A grill cart's side shelves can make it too wide for the gate after assembly; decide assembly location first. Skip a permanent gas run for an owner-supplied portable electric unit.

Related tasks include [Outdoor equipment](tasks.md#outdoor-equipment), [Assembly and anchoring](tasks.md#assembly-and-anchoring), [Fuel and combustion](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion), [Moving and haul away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away).

## Small appliances and countertop kits

Does the owner want countertop setup, an under-cabinet attachment or a built-in trim kit for the exact model?

Countertop: placement, plug-in and agreed demonstration. Approved built-in kit: confirm cutout, ventilation and receptacle before including fitting. No compatible kit: owner chooses another model or countertop placement.

Check whether a reservoir lid can lift or a service tray pull out under the upper cabinets. Skip drilling or cabinetry allowance for a straightforward plug-in handoff.
