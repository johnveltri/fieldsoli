# Deck and porch interview

“When you say redo the deck, what comes off on day one, what carries the new floor, and which door or stairs must the homeowner still be able to use?” Start at the contractor's handoff. A decking installer who receives released framing needs the layout, edge and rail-post details. A rebuild lead also needs responsibility for demolition, footings, house connection and the yard after excavation. For a single worn tread or loose rail, use the [localized repair playbook](carpentry.md#deck-board-stair-or-rail-repair). [Parent project](projects.md#porch-rebuild-or-deck-addition)

## Resurface on retained framing

| Clue | Question on site | Estimate decision and smaller tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Worn walking surface | Is the scope a wash and recoat, a few damaged boards, or removal and replacement of the entire walking surface? What happens to the removed material? | For refinishing, specify preparation, finish and faces coated. For board replacement, name the measured area, who supplies boards/fasteners, removal and disposal. Keep stair treads, fascia and skirting separate until included. [Cleaning](tasks.md#cleaning-and-seasonal-service) · [Coatings](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) · [Removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Hauling](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) |
| “The frame is good” | What has been inspected, including connections and bearing, and does the joist spacing suit the selected board and direction? | Confirmed sound and compatible → retain the structure. Sound framing with an unsupported picture-frame border or changed board direction → agree the layout or include the needed support as known work. If the underside is accessible, inspect before quoting. If exposure alone can reveal an agreed material issue, identify the affected member and approval step. A complete inspection that resolves it retires the condition. [Framing](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Deck surface](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) |
| Door, stairs or rails stay | What happens at the door threshold and top step with the new board thickness? Can boards be removed around retained posts, or must guard sections come off and go back? | Verified clearance and undisturbed posts → keep stairs and guards in place. If a rail must come off to achieve the promised finish, include agreed removal/reset and post connection checks; settle any stair-height correction before promising a board-only swap. Skip pricing new footings on a verified surface-only job. [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Stairs and rails](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) |
| Owner chose a photo or sample | Straight boards or perimeter border? Matching fascia and stair nosings or deck field only? | Record product, pattern, agreed exposed edges, supplier and any return for coating. A rough area covers only the named surface. A chosen border changes blocking and cuts; determine price coverage before adding it. [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork) · [Finish](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) |

## Rebuild from footings through rails

1. **Set limits and access.** Same footprint and door height, or a larger deck, roof or changed use? Who supplies the accepted plan, handles local permits/inspections and releases each stage? Mark stairs, planters and gates to stay, demolition limits, haul route, and whether the house door must be secured during the work. If this is the only entrance, settle alternate access before removal. [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Demolition and protection](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Hauling](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away)
2. **Set out footings.** Which old piers come out, which can remain in the ground, and who verifies the proposed locations, underground services, access for digging and spoil removal? Existing footings accepted by the responsible designer/authority → cite the accepted reuse scope. New footings → include the assigned layout, excavation, inspection before concealment as applicable, concrete/support installation, backfill and restoration. Use count and dimensions only when supplied for this job. Known poor access changes the plan now; buried obstruction that genuinely requires redesign calls for a stop, revised scope, price and approval before additional work. [Groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) · [Foundations](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Yard restoration](tasks.md#yard-and-irrigation)
3. **Define the structural and weather boundary.** Freestanding by design, or ledger attached at the house? For attached work, who verifies the receiving structure and owns flashing, disturbed siding and sealing? For a freestanding replacement of an attached deck, still assign closure of the old ledger location. Include agreed posts, beams, joists, connectors and blocking for the selected deck and guard system. A soft house rim exposed at removal is a different repair; establish capability and approval before promising it. [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Localized rot](carpentry.md#exterior-trim-and-localized-rot)
4. **Lay out the stairs from the finished surfaces.** Where exactly does the bottom step meet grade or the patio, and is its landing staying, being poured or supplied by another crew? Establish finished elevation and opening before confirming rise/run, stringers, treads, risers and stair supports. If the landing is unresolved, record the party and decision needed; a guessed stair count can change fabrication and rail lengths. [Stairs](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) · [Landing and paving](tasks.md#masonry-and-paving) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters)
5. **Separate deck guards, stair guards and graspable handrails.** Which edges/runs, sides of stairs, posts, infill, gates and handrail returns are on the plan? Confirm post attachment and blocking before covering framing. Carry linear feet by run only if known. A retained gate may need a new hinge/latch post when the stair opening moves. If lighting is included, assign power and wiring before the trim closes access. [Guards and rails](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) · [Gates](tasks.md#fences-and-gates) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls)
6. **Close the work.** Confirm decking, visible fasteners or concealed system, border, fascia/skirting and service access below. Separate deck field, stairs and rail finish so a supplied field labor figure stays attached to the field. Confirm who repairs disturbed siding, grades or seeds the access route, attends final inspection and returns access to the owner. [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork) · [Coatings](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) · [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff)

## Covered and screened porches

- **Floor under existing roof columns.** “Are those decorative wraps around separate posts, or do the columns land on the floor assembly we're taking out?” Verified independent supports → protect columns and define how floorboards and trim meet them. Shared or uncertain support → settle who designs and carries out temporary support and reinstatement before demolition. A new roof or enclosure needs its own support and footing details. [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork)
- **Exposure and water path.** Does wind-driven rain enter, and where does water leave at the sill, fascia and steps? Match the selected porch flooring to its exposure and installation instructions. If changing roof cover, identify roof-to-wall flashing, gutters, downspout discharge and repairs where the new work meets siding. Skip roof work for an untouched existing cover. [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks)
- **Screens, doors and rails.** Reuse removable screen panels or rebuild openings after post spacing changes? Reuse → label and store agreed panels. If columns will move, confirm who releases the restored openings for panel refitting and whether a return visit is needed. New openings → measure after framing, assign fabrication, fit the screen door and check swing, latch and threshold. Establish the separate guard detail for raised edges. A request to enclose a porch as living space needs its own approved use and envelope plan. [Screens](tasks.md#windows-and-screens) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Guards](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails)
