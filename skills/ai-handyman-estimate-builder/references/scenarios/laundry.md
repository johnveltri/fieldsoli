# Laundry installation

For work on the room or either end of a relocation, follow the [laundry room interview](laundry-room.md).

Open the entry for the actual purchase. Confirm model instructions and who owns the linked tasks before adding them to an estimate.

## Clothes washer

With the washer at final depth, where do the inlet hoses and drain terminate, and can the owner reach the valves?

Ready services: include model-specific unpacking, leveling, connection and fill/drain check. Inaccessible shutoffs or incompatible discharge: assign the needed service change or placement decision before setting.

Pedestal or drain pan height can move a top lid into a shelf. Check operating clearance if the photo shows overhead storage. Skip dryer vent questions for a standalone washer.

Related tasks include [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring).

## Vented dryer

What fuel and exhaust path does the selected unit need, and will the connector fit with the closet door shut?

Compatible existing services: scope agreed hookup, positioning and exhaust/operation check. Changed fuel or conflicting route: identify responsible trade, route and visit before scheduling.

Steam option may need a separate water feed; ask only if the owner wants it connected. Skip generic duct cleaning charges without an agreed cleaning scope.

Related tasks include [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort), [Fuel and combustion](tasks.md#fuel-and-combustion), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures).

## Ventless dryer or all-in-one

Where will condensate go for this model and can the owner reach its tank or filter at final position?

Tank configuration confirmed: include placement and show tank access. Plumbed discharge: confirm hose destination and include agreed connection/check. For a wash/dry combo also scope water and wash discharge.

A cabinet door can obstruct tank removal despite adequate machine depth. Skip adding an exterior exhaust route for a ventless unit.

Related tasks include [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff).

## Stacked laundry or laundry center

Is the owner replacing both units, one unit in a compatible pair, or a one-piece center? Who supplies the verified stacking kit?

Matched pair and kit on site: include agreed unstack, paired lift, reconnections and tests. Retaining an incompatible mate: owner selects a supported combination before scheduling. One-piece center: measure whole-unit carry path and connections.

If the washer changes, recheck the new dimensions before including door removal or a particular lift method.

A closet header may leave operating clearance but no room to lift the upper unit into place. Check assembly location. Skip charging for a second haul-away when one unit stays.

Related tasks include [Moving and haul away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away), [Assembly and anchoring](tasks.md#assembly-and-anchoring), [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort).
