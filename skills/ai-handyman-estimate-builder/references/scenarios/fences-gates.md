# Fences and gates

“Show me the gate dragging, the damaged section, and where you want the work to stop.” A bad gate and one broken bay can be two local repairs or the reason the homeowner wants a whole new fence. Ask which result they want before measuring or pricing a replacement. On a full replacement, start by settling the run endpoints and whether measured footage includes the gate opening. Once the extent is clear, ask about removal and access, then gate details where needed. When the contractor requests a draft before the later questions are answered, retain the measured price and mark unresolved fence material, height and gate configuration beside the installation lines for confirmation before acceptance.

| Part of the job | What to check with the contractor | What the answer changes |
|---|---|---|
| Gate diagnosis and repair | Watch the gate swing and latch. Is the hinge post moving at grade, are hinges loose or worn, or is the leaf out of square on a firm post? Does the bottom catch only where the ground rises? | Sound supports and hardware: square, brace and rehang the existing leaf if agreed. Failed hardware: name the parts to replace. Moving support: confirm post and footing work and the adjoining bay before promising a lasting adjustment. Correct a grade conflict only if contracted. [Fence and gate repairs](tasks.md#fences-and-gates) · [Groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) |
| Damaged section | Identify the bay between two posts. Check both supports, rails and individual boards. Which can stay? What material, size, pattern and finish can be matched? | Sound bay with broken parts: list only those rails, pickets or fasteners. Failed panel or support: measure the section and agree its tie-in to sound fence. For chain link distinguish fabric, tension fittings and posts; for vinyl or metal panels confirm compatible parts before promising a patch. Agree how new work will look next to weathered material. [Fences and gates](tasks.md#fences-and-gates) · [Coatings](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) |
| Replacement layout and access | Walk endpoints, corners, slope and changes of height. Who confirms the line and any neighbor access or local approval? Does measured length include the gate opening? What must stay enclosed during work? | Record each run, material, height, open or privacy style and agreed slope treatment. An old fence alone does not establish the property line. Assign access and temporary containment if needed. Separate fence footage from gate count; do not bill an opening twice. [Scope and scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) |
| Tear-out and disposal | Which fence, gate and posts come out? Must the old concrete be pulled or can it stay clear of new holes? What can get in to lift or carry it? | Identify demolition limits, footing removal, loading, disposal and hole backfill; distinguish stacking on site from hauling away. Group one agreed disposal scope across fence and gate. [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Haul away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) |
| Digging, posts and fence installation | Who arranges utility marking and checks private irrigation, lighting and pet lines? Are roots, paving or old concrete in the proposed post line? Confirm specified post and footing system, corners, gate supports and finish side. | Link excavation and setting posts to rails/panels or fabric, fasteners and ties to retained fence. Agree when the posts are ready to hang the gate. Identify paving or sprinkler work separately only when affected. For US digging, use the [811 guidance](https://811beforeyoudig.com/Before-You-Dig/) and confirm responses before excavation. | New gate and hardware | Single walk gate or double drive gate? Confirm the opening, required clear passage, swing, grade clearance, latch side, hinges and latch. Ask about a stop, drop rod or operator only if the design calls for it. | State the gate count, size basis, support posts and hardware included, and who supplies them. For an automated gate agree on the specialist, power and commissioning handoff. For a pool barrier confirm applicable barrier and latch requirements and uninterrupted protection before agreeing to alter it. Skip both branches for an ordinary manual yard gate. | Finish, yard and handoff | Bare, factory-finished or field-coated? What happens to holes, lawn, beds and paths? Who handles a later stain visit if one is needed? | Name the finish boundary and restoration promised: rough backfill, raking, seed and sod are different results. Walk the repaired section or new line; swing the gate through its travel, check clearances and verify it closes and latches. For a repair-only installer, settle the cause and the specific parts before enlarging scope. For a full installation, agree whether post setting, hardware, demolition, disposal and restoration are inside the installed rate or separate amounts. Keep fixed totals, per-foot rates and per-gate amounts at the level supplied. If a measurement is approximate, label the extension approximate and confirm the measurement before promising a total. If a concealed condition changes the agreed work, show the finding and get approval of the revised scope and price before proceeding.
