# Lawn care and landscaping interview

“Walk me from the front curb to the back gate. Which areas do they want maintained, what needs a first cleanup, and what should look different when you leave?” Identify the property or bed boundaries and who can provide access. Keep the first visit, recurring service and one-time installations distinct. A per-cut price belongs to the areas and service the contractor named. | Homeowner request | Question that changes the job | Effect on the estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Mow, trim and edge | Does the quote cover front and back, boulevard and slopes? Does the mower fit the narrowest gate? Does “edge” mean trim a lawn edge each visit or cut in a new bed line? | Define each visit in observable terms, including blowing off named hard surfaces, clipping collection if agreed, and gate closure. Record frequency, seasonal dates or weather arrangements only as agreed. Initial overgrowth or a new bed edge needs its own agreed scope and price coverage. [Yard work](tasks.md#yard-and-irrigation) · [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) |
| Spring or fall cleanup, hedge trimming | Which beds, shrubs and leaf piles? Are beds being cleared back to soil or are selected plants and leaf cover staying? Where can debris go? | Mark retained plants and agreed pruning limits. Distinguish curb staging from bagging, loading, disposal and another visit after leaf drop. Identify a tree-service handoff for overhead or unstable limbs. [Seasonal service](tasks.md#cleaning-and-seasonal-service) · [Hauling](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) |
| Bed edging, mulch, planting or raised beds | Is the owner refreshing existing beds or changing outlines and removing turf? What depth, material, plant size and watering arrangement have they selected? | Measure bed area and agreed applied depth before calculating volume. Assign weeding, sod removal, delivery, wheelbarrow access, edging, planting and final cleanup only as applicable. Record who buys substitutions and who waters after installation. Skip irrigation design when the owner has accepted hand watering. [Yard and irrigation](tasks.md#yard-and-irrigation) · [Access and groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) |
| Patch bare turf, overseed, aerate or lay sod | Is this one worn patch or a whole lawn? Does water stand there, and can water reach it after planting? | Sound area with a selected treatment → name the area, preparation, seed or sod, installation and aftercare owner. Recurring ponding or a traffic path → agree on the intended repair before promising establishment. Separate follow-up visits and treatments from the installation. [Yard](tasks.md#yard-and-irrigation) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters) |
| Sprinkler or drip repair | One broken head, an entire dry zone or a new bed outside the existing coverage? Can the contractor run the zone with the owner before quoting? | Verified broken head → replace the agreed part and test coverage. Whole zone fails → scope diagnosis before assigning trenching or valve replacement. For new runs, assign layout, excavation, testing and turf restoration. [Irrigation](tasks.md#yard-and-irrigation) · [Water supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) |
| Regrade, drain or add a path, patio or driveway | Where does runoff enter and where can it discharge? Which elevations at doors, gates or adjacent properties constrain the design? What below-ground work crosses the proposed paving? | Confirm outlet, utility locations, access and spoil destination before promising grade changes. Install agreed sleeves, drains and lighting routes before final paving. Assign base preparation, paver or concrete finish, joint edges, restored turf and final walkthrough. Refer retaining-wall design or inaccessible excavation to the agreed specialist. For a mixed job, identify when routine service begins on the renovated area and who maintains it until then. Keep an owner’s rough lawn dimensions approximate. Confirm whether a quoted service price is per visit, per season, or for the whole installation before showing any total.
