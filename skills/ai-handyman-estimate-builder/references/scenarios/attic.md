# Attic conversion interview

“What will the owner take upstairs, how often will they go up there, and what are you handing back?” Mark the intended storage platform, service walkway, sleeping area or bathroom on the floor plan. Keep the quoted surface and its units attached to the actual layer: platform decking, structural subfloor, finished bedroom floor and bathroom tile are separate deliverables. Ask only about the branch and trade handoffs the contractor owns. [Parent project](projects.md#attic-conversion-or-insulation-retrofit) · [Scope and scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling)

## Storage platform or service walkway

| Clue | Question on the walk | Effect on the estimate |
|---|---|---|
| “Lay some plywood so we can store things” | Seasonal bins, heavy shelving, or just a path to the air handler? Which marked area and intended load has the supporting framing been verified for? | Confirmed storage area → specify its boundaries, intended use and decking detail. Walkway only → quote the route and service area. Unknown capacity → assign verification before promising storage or adding reinforcement. Existing boards or visible truss chords alone cannot establish capacity. [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) |
| Blown insulation and vented eaves | Can the specified platform sit above the insulation at full depth while keeping the eave air paths open? Which junction boxes, shutoffs or equipment panels will still need access? | Verified raised detail → include only the agreed supports, decking and access panels. Interference → settle elevation, a smaller footprint or a removable section before quoting. If someone else builds the supports, start decking at their handoff. When the contractor prices only laying sheets, confirm whether fitting a removable panel falls within that figure. [Insulation and air sealing](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls) |
| Hall closet hatch or folding ladder | Can the intended bins, decking sections and people pass through the clear opening, with space below to deploy the ladder? | Fit verified → protect the hall and retain the agreed access. Different access needed → settle location, framing approval, displaced wiring, casing and ceiling repair before promising a larger opening. Confirm who reinstates the insulated hatch cover. Skip bedroom and plumbing questions for a confirmed seasonal storage job. ## Bedroom and bathroom conversion

1. **Lay out the rooms at finished heights.** Mark where the bed, stair landing, shower and vanity users will actually stand. Does the plan account for the new floor layers, sloping ceiling finish and low sidewalls at those points? A generous ridge height alone cannot settle shower or stair headroom. Confirm who owns local approvals, usable room dimensions and the designated sleeping-room escape opening. An accepted layout releases the relevant work. If a dormer or different stair location is still under review, keep affected work and quantities provisional. [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Stairs and rails](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) · [Windows](tasks.md#windows-and-screens)
2. **Establish support and access.** Who verified floor loads, the stair opening, any roof changes and the load path to supports below? Confirmed framing ready → quote the assigned floor layer only. Specified reinforcement → assign framing, any opening in occupied rooms below, temporary protection and restoration before finishes. Keep proposed cuts to trusses or joists open until there is a design. For permanent stairs, identify lower-floor footprint, landing, headroom, guard and handrail owners and how the old hatch will be treated. [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Stair and rail work](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) · [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Walls and ceilings](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings)
3. **Make the roof and room enclosure ready.** Existing accepted gable opening → specify protection, casing and operation check. New dormer or roof opening → assign framing, window, flashing, roof/siding tie-ins, temporary weather protection and interior returns. Ask where the insulation and air barrier run across slopes, knee walls and the remaining cold eaves. Use the selected vented or unvented assembly and retain service access to equipment outside the room. Signs of leakage → establish who finds and corrects the source before enclosing that area. [Envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Insulation](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Water intrusion](tasks.md#water-intrusion-and-recovery) · [Windows](tasks.md#windows-and-screens)
4. **Trace bathroom services down through the house.** Is the planned bathroom over a verified usable stack, and can the waste pipe reach it along an accepted route through or around the framing? Aligning rooms on a sketch does not establish a route. Verified connection → assign supplies, waste, vent, rough-in and testing. Obstructed route → settle a redesigned layout or chase and who opens, closes and paints the ceiling or wall below. Confirm who makes and flashes any new vent penetration and who owns the bath fan's exterior duct and termination. [Water supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Paint](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings)
5. **Release concealed work before closure.** Identify who provides bedroom heating/cooling and air circulation, lights, switches, receptacles and applicable alarms. Establish the order for services routed below the subfloor, insulation and rough-work checks before fixing panels or hanging drywall. Confirm when the path must remain open for carrying in large items such as a tub or shower base. [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Alarms](tasks.md#technology-and-alarms) · [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff)
6. **Finish the two rooms and the route downstairs.** Carry bedroom flooring and bathroom substrate, waterproofing and tile by their actual surfaces. Identify shower assembly/test owner, fixture supply and final plumbing, then assign bedroom doors, knee-wall access doors, trim, paint, stair-opening repairs and walkthrough. For a finish-only installer, note the released starting surface and stop at their agreed handoff. If the owner wants insulation and occasional equipment access only, establish who seals the hatch and provides the service route. Ask about reinforcing or decking a larger storage area only when the intended use calls for it.
