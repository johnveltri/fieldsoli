# Basement remodel interview

“What’s downstairs now, what will the family use it for afterward, and which part are you handing back?” An existing rec room getting new flooring can stop at the stair landing. A new bedroom and bath introduce decisions under the slab, outside the foundation wall and above the finished ceiling. A finish carpenter on that build may only own the work after those decisions are made. Start with the relevant branch and follow the answers in rounds. [Parent project](projects.md#basement-finishing) · [Scope and scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling)

## Refresh an existing finished basement

| Walkthrough clue | Ask | Change to the estimate |
|---|---|---|
| “Freshen up downstairs” | Which rooms, stair surfaces, ceilings and utility spaces are included? Which finishes stay? Who moves contents and keeps the route upstairs clear? | Rec room only → describe the named room and stopping edge. Stairs included → measure treads/risers separately and settle nosing, trim and handrail finish. Assign furniture moves, protection and haul-away once. [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Moving](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) · [Stairs and rails](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) |
| Carpet to plank over slab | What have you verified about the slab for this exact floor, and what will the finished height do at the bottom step, door and utility threshold? | Verified compatible, dry and flat enough → include agreed removal, floor, base and transitions. Accessible mismatch → agree preparation or a different finish before quoting. Bare slab hidden under glued carpet → decide how ordinary prep is covered and what specific discovery would need measured repair and price approval. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork) |
| Musty baseboard, stain or past flooding | When was it last wet, and who verified the source was corrected and surfaces are ready to cover? | Repaired and released → include the defined restoration. Active or unexplained wet area → identify investigation/repair owner and defer affected finish. A stain alone establishes neither mold nor a failed foundation. [Water intrusion and recovery](tasks.md#water-intrusion-and-recovery) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters) |
| Patch walls or change ceiling | Touch up named patches, paint entire walls, replace selected suspended tiles, or close the ceiling? Which valves, cleanouts and junctions must remain accessible? | Matching tiles and grid stay → count only the replacements. New closed ceiling → agree access panel positions, light work and finish extent. Skip window-well excavation questions on an unchanged family-room repaint. [Walls and ceilings](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings) · [Paint](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) |

## Build a bedroom and bathroom from an unfinished shell

1. **Confirm bedroom feasibility before promising a bedroom.** Who owns the local plan review, permit and inspection arrangements? Is the designated sleeping-room escape opening accepted for this layout, including operation, well and path outside? Existing accepted opening → protect it and agree interior returns. New or unverified opening → identify survey/measurement, design, excavation, foundation cut, window, well drainage, exterior restoration and interior casing owners. Keep approval as a prerequisite until established. Measure stair and ceiling clearance at the *finished* floor and the lowest beam or duct. A check made at the bare slab can miss the controlling height. [Windows](tasks.md#windows-and-screens) · [Excavation](tasks.md#groundwork) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Stairs](tasks.md#decks-stairs-and-rails) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters)
2. **Release the shell for enclosure.** Ask what moisture history, slab checks and drainage provisions inform the proposed wall/floor assembly. For new living space, establish who checks radon and accounts for any existing mitigation system or needed work. Confirm who selects and installs the below-grade insulation/air-sealing assembly. Known water entry or an unassessed issue affecting that assembly stays open before enclosing it. Dry, assessed shell → carry only agreed preparation. [Moisture and repair](tasks.md#water-intrusion-and-recovery) · [Insulation and air sealing](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring)
3. **Locate the bath before cutting the slab.** “Has the plumber verified where those capped pipes go, their elevations, and whether they serve the selected toilet, vanity and shower positions?” Usable gravity rough-in → assign verified tie-ins and fixture setting. Missing/misaligned rough-in → identify who locates services, opens the slab, runs supply/waste/vent, tests before cover and restores the slab and disturbed layers. If gravity discharge is unconfirmed, ask who decides between gravity and a sewage ejector. A sump for groundwater does not establish a sewage connection. If an ejector is specified, assign pit, service access, power and commissioning to the agreed parties. [Water supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Slab repair](tasks.md#masonry-and-paving) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls)
4. **Frame rooms around systems that stay.** Ask where partitions, bedroom door, closet, soffits and mechanical-room access land. Confirm how the furnace filter, water heater, panel, shutoffs and sump cover can be serviced or replaced after enclosure. A removable panel may serve a valve but leave no path for equipment replacement. Plan accepted → include named framing, doors, trim and access detail. Interference → settle a larger door, removable section or revised layout before framing. Distinguish partition framing from a structural alteration. [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork) · [Walls and ceilings](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings)
5. **Settle rough-ins and release for closure.** Who provides bedroom heat and air circulation with its door shut, bath exhaust to outdoors, lighting, receptacles, controls and applicable alarms? Existing service verified adequate → include only agreed extensions. Changed ducts/penetrations → assign mechanical design, exterior seal and finish repair. Name the trade responsible for testing and who confirms insulation and rough-ins are released before drywall. [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Exterior envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Alarms](tasks.md#technology-and-alarms)
6. **Split bathroom finish work at real handoffs.** Half bath → toilet, basin, finishes and agreed connections. Full bath → choose tub, prefabricated shower or tiled assembly before promising waterproofing and tile. Establish who supplies fixtures, owns pan and drain junction, checks the assembly before cover, sets fixtures and returns to leak-test. Where another installer hands over a tested assembly, start tile there. [Bathroom interview](bathroom.md) · [Fixture interviews](fixtures.md) · [Tile and wet assemblies](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies) · [Backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing)
7. **Measure and hand back the rooms.** Separate bedroom/rec-room flooring from bathroom floor and shower walls. Name trim, door swing, ceiling finish, paint limits and stair transition. After agreed releases, assign final fixture, fan and alarm checks, window operation and owner walkthrough. An existing flooring labor figure applies only to its stated rooms and units. Do not add owner-contracted trades or infer occupancy approval. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Paint](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) · [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff)

For a finish-only bid ask what the GC or homeowner will hand over: dry and framed shell, rough work released, or finished walls ready for flooring. Record prerequisites once. If bedroom approval, bath drain design or another trade's price is undecided, keep it visible without inventing a charge or turning it into promised contractor work.
