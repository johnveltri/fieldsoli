# Marketplace bookings in homeowner language

When a callout minimum covers mounting several items, establish what the first paid hour buys if the spot lacks sound support. The customer draft can include checking the points, installing where feasible, and reviewing options for the rest before more time or wall work is approved. Confirm any unsettled minimum-charge terms with the contractor.

A listing's selected category may describe only one part of the request. Use the description, photos and promised outcome to pick the branch. Preserve any fixed booking terms exactly as the contractor gives them.
