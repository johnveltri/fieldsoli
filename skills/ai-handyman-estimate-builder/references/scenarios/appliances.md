# Appliance installation and replacement

Start with the actual appliance, exact model if selected, and the promised handoff. A delivery-only booking ends at a different point from removal, connection and a successful operating test. Compare the model's instructions with accessible site conditions. Ask about the carrying route, old unit, packaging or utility work only when it affects this job. Confirm who supplies missing kits, who makes connections and when a specialist must have finished. Measurements from a different model are a poor basis for a fit promise.

## Multi-appliance job

For a remodel, use the [kitchen interview](kitchen.md) to settle cabinet, counter and utility handoffs before installation.

For “install the kitchen package,” identify each model, the delivery team's promised handoff and the connections this contractor owns. Track any supplied bundle price once. Choose the appliance names below for their specific questions.

- “Will delivery remove the old dishwasher and range, or only leave the new units in the garage?” Curbside delivery can add a measured carry route and agreed removal work; full retailer installation can leave only cabinet trim and owner orientation. Assign [lifting and haul-away](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) once for the items actually included.
- “The floor is going in Friday. Can we still get the old dishwasher out, and will the refrigerator roll into its opening afterward?” Verified clearance keeps one agreed installation visit. A trapped machine may need removal before flooring and a return for hookup. Confirm timing and price coverage before promising the two visits. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Cabinets](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters)
- “Can the other units go in if the oven cabinet isn't ready?” If yes, identify which units reach working order on visit one and who releases the oven opening for the return. If the owner wants one visit, make cabinet completion a scheduling prerequisite. Preserve the supplied package price without assigning invented portions to either visit. [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff)

## Choose the model or installation type

| Appliance or request | Question that changes the estimate | How to use the answer |
|---|---|---|
| [Built-in dishwasher](kitchen-water.md#built-in-dishwasher) | Has finish flooring trapped the old feet behind a lip? Where can the new unit be secured under this counter? | Clear opening and compatible attachment support a swap and fill/drain test. Trapped unit or incompatible mounting needs an agreed extraction or mounting plan first. |
| [Portable or drawer dishwasher](kitchen-water.md#portable-or-drawer-dishwasher) | Faucet-coupled portable unit or drawers inside cabinetry? | Verify faucet adapter and storage route for portable. For drawers, confirm opening, front panels and service access before quoting a built-in install. |
| [Refrigerator or freezer](kitchen-water.md#refrigerator-or-freezer) | Will the doors open far enough against the return wall to withdraw bins? Is water or ice included? | Include moving, leveling and the agreed existing-line connection. Resolve swing, delivery disassembly or new supply before promising full use. |
| [Panel-ready refrigerator, wine or beverage cooler](kitchen-water.md#panel-ready-refrigerator-wine-or-beverage-cooler) | Is it rated for this enclosure, and who supplies panel, hinge hardware and ventilation opening? | Use a cabinet coordination line for an integrated model. A freestanding placement can omit panel work. |
| [Standalone ice maker](kitchen-water.md#standalone-ice-maker) | Does this model need a drain as well as a supply, and is the required route available? | Confirm gravity or pump arrangement from the selected model before quoting an in-place hookup. |
| [Electric or induction range](cooking.md#electric-or-induction-range) | Does the model match the available supply and space, including anti-tip attachment and the drawer/cord space behind it? | If ready, include setting, securing and operation check. Assign unresolved supply changes before scheduling. |
| [Gas or dual-fuel range](cooking.md#gas-or-dual-fuel-range) | Are fuel type, shutoff, electric supply and final setback compatible, and who is making the gas connection? | Confirm the connection and test owner. A fuel change or conversion needs its own defined scope. |
| [Cooktop or downdraft unit](cooking.md#cooktop-or-downdraft-unit) | Does the cutout fit on all four sides and clear drawers, oven and duct below? | Confirm drop-in fit or obtain an approved counter/duct modification plan before fabrication or removal. |
| [Wall oven, double oven or microwave drawer](cooking.md#wall-oven-double-oven-or-microwave-drawer) | Does the cabinet provide the specified support, depth and service access? Can the unit be lifted into place along this route? | Scope supported placement and trim fit or assign cabinet preparation and handling before delivery. |
| [Over-the-range microwave](cooking.md#over-the-range-microwave) | Does the selected mounting template match the upper cabinet, and where does exhaust go? Can the hinge clear the side wall? | Verify backing, bracket and recirculation kit or duct alignment. Identify patching or duct work when the outlet moves. |
| [Range hood or insert](cooking.md#range-hood-or-insert) | Is the hood recirculating, connected to an existing exterior duct or part of a new exhaust design? | Confirm filters for recirculation. Assign duct, termination, cabinet cut and any mechanical design for a changed route. |
| [Clothes washer](laundry.md#clothes-washer) | Will hoses, valves and discharge remain accessible at final depth, and where will the first drain cycle discharge? | Include model-specific unpacking, leveling and agreed fill/drain test. Settle valve or drain changes before the move. |
| [Vented dryer](laundry.md#vented-dryer) | Which fuel and vent route are present, and will pushing the unit back crush the connection? | Verify connections and route; scope a cleanout, fuel handoff or reroute only when established. Steam models can add a water connection. |
| [Ventless dryer or all-in-one](laundry.md#ventless-dryer-or-all-in-one) | Does this model collect condensate or discharge it, and can the owner reach the tank/filter? | Select tank access or confirmed drain path. Omit an exterior duct installation for a ventless model. |
| [Stacked laundry or laundry center](laundry.md#stacked-laundry-or-laundry-center) | Is it one assembly or two compatible units with a specified kit? Can one be serviced or extracted past the closet door? | Confirm kit, lift assistance, clearance and reconnection of the retained unit if only one changes. |
| [Garbage disposer or instant hot dispenser](kitchen-water.md#garbage-disposer-or-instant-hot-dispenser) | Will discharge align with the trap, is a dishwasher attached, and is the intended switch or tap opening available? | For disposer, scope the drain interface and agreed leak/drain test. For dispenser, confirm separate tank, tap, supply and power space. |
| [Window or portable AC](specialty-appliances.md#window-or-portable-ac) | What window style and opening will take the unit, bracket or hose kit, and who removes it at season end? | Confirm support, sealing and seasonal handoff; route split-system line sets to the HVAC scope. |
| [Water heater, softener or filter](specialty-appliances.md#water-heater-softener-or-filter) | Are connections, bypass, drain, vent or condensate route appropriate to this equipment and still accessible for service? | Separate cartridge service from a whole-unit change. Assign supply, fuel, electrical and commissioning work to the agreed parties. |
| [Outdoor grill, pizza oven or patio heater](specialty-appliances.md#outdoor-grill-pizza-oven-or-patio-heater) | Portable kit or fixed installation on a prepared base with a permanent fuel supply? | Scope assembly and placement for a kit; confirm fuel, weather exposure, clearances and test owner for a built-in. |
| [Small appliances and countertop kits](specialty-appliances.md#small-appliances-and-countertop-kits) | Countertop placement, faucet adapter, under-cabinet mounting or a listed trim kit? | A plug-in setup may end at demonstration. Verify mounting, ventilation and power before promising enclosure work. |
