# Bathroom remodel interview

For a bathroom under a sloped roof, use the [attic conversion interview](attic.md) to check finished headroom and the drain route through the floor below.

For a new bath below grade, start with the [basement build-out](basement.md#build-a-bedroom-and-bathroom-from-an-unfinished-shell) to settle the drain route and slab handoff.

Start with the room the homeowner wants back and which part the contractor has promised. For “refresh the hall bath,” ask what comes out and what must still be usable during work. For “tile the plumber's new shower,” start at the surface the plumber will hand over. Use the following branches as the conversation reaches them.

## Refresh in the existing layout

- **Keep or disturb the wet area.** “When you say tile the bath, does the new tile stop at the tub apron, or are you removing the surround too?” Floor only → identify the measured floor, threshold and agreed fixture resets; keep the tub and surround in place. Surround too → follow the tub-flange, substrate and waterproofing handoff before pricing tile. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Tile and wet assemblies](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies)
- **Vanity footprint.** “Can you see whether the floor and backsplash continue under the smaller vanity?” Verified finished → include the swap and specified connections. Known bare strip → agree on a wider cabinet, a defined infill with matched material, or an accepted visible boundary. Accessible but unchecked → contractor check before quoting. Skip faucet-hole questions once the matched sink and faucet are confirmed. [Vanity and faucet](fixtures.md) · [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters)
- **Paint and accessories.** “After the wider mirror and towel bar come down, which wall gets painted?” Same coverage → include only agreed holes and touch-up. Exposed outline and full-wall finish requested → include patch, prime and paint to named corners. Resolve paint supply before sending. [Wall repair and finish](surfaces.md) · [Mounting](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing)

## Gut remodel or fixture relocation

- **Only working bath.** “Can the toilet stay out between demolition and the flooring cure, or does someone need it overnight?” Alternate bath available → plan the agreed single final reset. Overnight use needed → establish a workable temporary arrangement, cure constraints and who owns each visit before promising service. Do not turn a known access constraint into a discovery surcharge. [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal) · [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling)
- **Layout and open walls.** “Where will the new drain land, and from which side can the plumber reach it?” Same position with confirmed access → scope in-place connections. Move through ceiling below → identify who opens, reroutes, tests, closes, matches texture and paints the named ceiling. A joist conflict that needs design stays open until the responsible party settles it. [Drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Supply](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Wall and ceiling closure](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings)
- **Fan, lights and control.** “Is the fan a replacement on a verified exterior duct, or does someone have to create the route? Can the housing be reached from the room?” Confirmed route and fit → include the agreed fan work and operation check. New route → assign duct, exterior termination, weather seal and ceiling repair before closing. A separate light or heated floor may change controls and power; assign that design/connection only if in this project. [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope)
- **Hidden damage after removal.** Visible soft floor → settle repair or assessment now. Stained but inaccessible area → ask where base prep ends. If the contractor confirms exposed soft subfloor is extra, show the affected area, measure it, quote repair and wait for homeowner approval before covering. If framing is affected beyond their scope, stop there and agree who investigates. Retire the condition if inspection confirms a sound surface. [Moisture and recovery](tasks.md#water-intrusion-and-recovery)

## Tub replacement, shower conversion or accessible entry

- **Tub versus shower base.** “Are we keeping the tub, setting a replacement in the same alcove, or building a shower at floor level?” Retained tub → settle protection and how new wall waterproofing meets its flange. New tub → verify delivery around door turns, drain handing, alcove fit and test access before wall closure. Floor-level shower → establish entry elevation, drain location, slope, adjoining floor and support detail before promising a flush transition. [Doors](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Tile assembly](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies)
- **Who hands over what.** “When you arrive to tile, who owns the pan, waterproofing at the drain and penetrations, and the check that releases it for tile?” Tested assembly by another installer → start tile scope at their handoff. Contractor owns assembly → include agreed substrate, system, transitions and required verification before tile. Ownership unresolved → flag before sending; assigning both contractors the membrane double-counts work. Use system-specific timing. [Shower system water testing](https://www.schluter.com/schluter-us/en_US/faq/shower-system-water-test-how-to)
- **Backing, access and finish.** “Has the resident confirmed the bar and seat positions, and who needs backing before the wall closes?” Confirmed positions → scope agreed blocking and record locations for the installer. To be chosen → owner decision before closure. If glass is custom, settle who templates the finished opening and returns after fabrication. Verify the door clears the vanity and the water controls are reachable from the agreed entry. Skip glass questions for a confirmed curtain-only job. [Accessibility](tasks.md#access-and-safety-fittings) · [Backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing)
- **Threshold and fit-off.** “At finished height, can the bathroom door swing, and who resets the toilet and trims the floor edge?” Confirmed clear → fit the agreed transition, reset and test. Known conflict → agree door adjustment, threshold or floor build-up before ordering. Confirm which trades return for valves, fixtures, sealing and leak checks, and who tells the homeowner when the shower is ready for use. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Fixture handoff](fixtures.md) · [Final walkthrough](tasks.md#handoff)

## Tile-only or finish-only subcontract

Ask “What condition will the room be in on your first day and exactly where will you leave it for the next person?” If tile starts on a completed, released shower, include measured surfaces, layout/edge treatment, grout and agreed handoff. If the installer receives bare framing, settle the assembly owner first. Keep contractor-supplied quantities attached to the actual surface (shower walls, pan or room floor). Skip whole-room demolition and fixture procurement when another party owns them.
