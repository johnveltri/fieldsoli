# Garage conversion interview

“When the work is done, what will someone do here on an ordinary day? Will cars still come through the big door, will someone operate tools, or will someone live here with their own kitchen and bath?” Establish whether the garage is attached or detached, which bays change use, and what the contractor has promised to hand over. Apply the workshop questions to a working garage and the dwelling questions to an ADU. A drywall subcontractor can begin at the agreed wall-closure handoff. ## Finish a workshop in the existing garage

| What the contractor finds | Ask on the walk | Effect on the estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Owner wants benches and tool storage | Which machines or materials come through the door? Where do long boards travel, the bench drawers open and the overhead door and tracks move? | Verified layout → include the agreed bench dimensions, backing and anchor points. Conflict → settle a shorter bench or different rack location before fabrication. Assign moving stored items and clearing the work area. [Mounting and backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing) · [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters) · [Moving](tasks.md#moving-and-haul-away) |
| “Finish the walls” | Which bays, walls and ceilings? Are walls open for blocking, wiring or insulation? What will the owner see at handoff? | Paint-ready drywall → include the agreed board and finish extent. Painted walls or a plywood tool wall → assign materials, finish and blocking explicitly. If a vehicle bay remains alongside the shop, verify the specified separation and penetrations before promising openings. [Walls and ceilings](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings) · [Insulation](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Paint](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings) |
| Table saw, welder, compressor or dust collection | What equipment will actually run together, what power is already confirmed, and where does dust or exhaust go? | Verified existing outlets and portable collection → scope only agreed equipment placement. New circuit, fixed duct or heat → assign electrician/mechanical work, outlet or duct locations and release before closing walls. Skip household appliance questions for a dry workbench. [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) |
| Sloping slab or seasonal water at overhead door | Keep the existing bare floor, prepare for coating, or build a level platform for specific equipment? Can runoff still leave at the door? | Accepted slab → keep the finish boundary at walls or bench. Coating requested → verify product suitability, prep, moisture history and downtime. Wet threshold → establish drainage repair owner before coating. Check operator travel with storage in place at handoff. ## Convert the garage to an ADU with kitchen and bath

1. **Confirm the use and layout.** Who owns local planning, permits, inspections and final sign-off? Check the entry, sleeping layout and escape opening, finished headroom and separation from the house or a retained parking bay against the accepted plan. Approved layout → carry the contractor's portion of it. Unresolved use or opening → keep the decision visible before promising an occupiable unit. Local requirements and service arrangements depend on the address. [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling) · [Windows](tasks.md#windows-and-screens) · [Entry and accessibility](tasks.md#access-and-safety-fittings)
2. **Replace the garage opening.** Who takes down the overhead door, operator and tracks, and who makes their power safe? Does the new wall have confirmed bearing at the old vehicle threshold? Assign the infill framing, window or entry door, sill flashing, exterior siding/trim and interior returns. If the apron drains toward the infill, settle weather protection and grade at the new threshold before quoting finish work. If another crew hands over a dried-in shell, begin there. [Door removal](tasks.md#garage-doors-and-operators) · [Structure](tasks.md#foundations-and-structure) · [Entry door](tasks.md#doors-and-locks) · [Envelope](tasks.md#exterior-envelope) · [Drainage](tasks.md#drainage-and-gutters)
3. **Trace utilities before settling the floor.** Where do power, water, hot water, sanitary waste and venting reach the future rooms? For a detached garage, trace the route back to the house or utility connection. Verified existing services → scope the agreed extensions. Unknown sewer elevation, panel capacity or route → assign verification before committing to a pump, upgrade or trench. Where cutting is established, allocate locating, excavation, trade installation, tests, backfill, slab reinstatement and disturbed paving/yard. Avoid counting the shared trench separately in kitchen and bath. [Groundwork](tasks.md#groundwork) · [Water](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures) · [Waste](tasks.md#waste-and-drains) · [Power](tasks.md#power-and-controls) · [Paving repair](tasks.md#masonry-and-paving)
4. **Set the finished floor and close the shell.** How does the proposed moisture, insulation and leveling detail affect door thresholds, headroom, shower entry and cabinet heights at opposite ends of the sloped slab? Confirm the specified assembly or leave it open for design. Assign partitions, air sealing, heating, cooling, ventilation, lights and alarms. Identify who releases concealed work before drywall, then define paint, flooring and trim by room. [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring) · [Insulation](tasks.md#insulation-and-air-sealing) · [Ventilation](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort) · [Alarms](tasks.md#technology-and-alarms) · [Walls](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings) · [Trim](tasks.md#trim-and-millwork)
5. **Kitchen handoffs.** Which appliances are selected and who verifies openings, service locations and hood route before cabinets go in? Establish who fits cabinets, releases bases for template, installs counters and mounts the sink, then who returns for plumbing, appliance connections and tests. Record retailer work only for the units it covers. [Kitchen interview](kitchen.md) · [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters) · [Appliance suite](appliances.md#multi-appliance-job)
6. **Bathroom handoffs.** Which fixtures, where do their drains land, and is the shower prefabricated or tiled? Confirm who installs the pan, makes the drain connection, waterproofs and tests the assembly before cover. Allocate bath fan duct/termination, tile, vanity, toilet, final connections and testing. Compare the bath floor quantity to any living-area floor allowance before combining. [Bathroom interview](bathroom.md) · [Wet assembly](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies) · [Vanity and toilet](fixtures.md)
7. **Hand back the agreed portion.** Identify who demonstrates heat, ventilation, water, fixtures and appliances, finishes exterior access and arranges any final inspection. A wall or flooring labor quote can cover the agreed surfaces inside an ADU without absorbing the cost of separate trades. [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff) · [Scheduling](tasks.md#scope-and-scheduling)

A workshop sink alone does not settle the intended use. Ask whether it serves shop cleanup or is part of independent household cooking and bathing. For a utility sink in an otherwise dry shop, add only its actual supply/drain, installation and test responsibilities.
