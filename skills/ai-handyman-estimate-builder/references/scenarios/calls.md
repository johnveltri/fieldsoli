# Homeowner calls that cross trade boundaries

Start with the symptom and what the contractor has actually agreed to provide. A homeowner may call a generalist first. Ask who will diagnose, isolate or make safe, repair, restore access openings and verify the result. One person may own several steps. For work dependent on qualifications, permit or inspection, establish the location, local requirements and responsible party before committing it to the estimate. Urgent active hazards belong with the appropriate emergency service or utility before routine estimating.

| What the homeowner says | Decision-changing question | Possible estimate outcome |
|---|---|---|
| “The ceiling under the bath is brown.” | Is the source repaired and drying complete, or is the stain spreading? | Quote a defined patch and paint after release, or scope investigation/access and leave closure pending. |
| “My kitchen breaker trips.” | Which device or loads are affected, and has anyone identified the fault? | Document symptoms and arrange electrical diagnosis. Restore any specified access openings after repair. A new breaker is an unsupported diagnosis. |
| “Water backs up when I run the washer.” | Is only the standpipe affected or do other fixtures back up? | Separate hose/standpipe assessment from a shared drain, sewer or septic investigation. |
| “We have no water.” | One tap, the whole house, or also neighbors? Well or municipal supply? | Fixture service, building shutoff/supply assessment, utility or well specialist are different visits. |
| “The AC won't cool.” | Is this a window unit install problem or a central system failure? | Verify fitting or controls within scope; assign equipment diagnosis before committing to replacement or refrigerant work. |
| “Chimney leaks” or “fireplace smells.” | Is the request exterior water-entry repair or a combustion/venting assessment? | Route flashing and interior damage separately from flue or appliance inspection. |
| “Door sticks and the wall cracked.” | Is this one hinge or has the opening changed along with nearby floors or doors? | Quote an established hardware issue. For signs of movement, establish who assesses support before trimming doors or filling cracks. |
| “Garage door is hanging crooked.” | Is it an accessory/remote issue or is the moving assembly visibly out of position? | Route spring, cable or track fault to the responsible door technician. Price trim/seals only when the door works. |
| “My refrigerator leaks.” | Is the leak traced to the supply connection, the appliance or still unknown? | Scope a verified connection or assign appliance diagnosis. Separate wet-floor restoration. |
| “Replace this gas stove; I smell gas.” | Is the odor active now? | Direct active odor to immediate utility/emergency assistance from a safe location. Resume replacement scoping after the site is cleared. |
| “Install this ceiling fan where the light was.” | Has the support been verified for this fan, and are its controls compatible? | Confirm the fixture swap or assign backing/wiring work before quoting closure and finish. |
| “There's mold in the basement.” | Where is moisture entering, how far has it spread and who will assess affected finishes? | Assign source investigation and remediation responsibility before agreeing to rebuild. |
| “The roof leaks after rain.” | Who will establish the entry point and who owns temporary protection? | Separate protection, confirmed roofing/flashing repair, drying and finish restoration. A ceiling spot alone cannot locate the roof defect. |
| “Can you take out this wall?” | Is there an approved structural detail, and what services cross it? | Map design, support, reroutes, floor infill and finish matching before offering demolition or a finish-only package. |
| “Outlet for my EV / backup generator / solar battery.” | Is the contract for physical mounting, electrical design and connection, utility coordination, or all of them? | Define the equipment pad or backing, service work and final commissioning owner separately. |
| “Get rid of scratching noises / a soft tree limb over my roof.” | Is there an active animal, unstable limb or damage already assessed? | Coordinate wildlife or tree service first where needed, then quote a specific closure or surface repair. |
| “Fix my treadmill / recliner / pool table.” | Is this assembly, an in-home move, adjustment or a failed component? | Verify model, parts and serviceability; separate carry/disassembly and reassembly from mechanical or electronic repair. |
| “The sump runs constantly.” | Is water entering, recirculating at the discharge, or is a control issue established? | Scope drainage assessment or verified pump work and define who tests discharge; keep basement refinishing separate. |
