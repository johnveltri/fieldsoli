# Larger projects and the work they can contain

Start at the homeowner’s requested result. A linked task is a possible work package. Identify who owns it, what needs to happen before it, and whether it belongs in this quote. Follow only branches that change a decision. For shared demolition, mobilization, finish work or testing, assign it once across packages.

## Bathroom remodel

Use the [bathroom interview](bathroom.md) to turn the relevant work packages into estimate lines.

Does the layout stay? Moving the toilet or converting a tub moves drain and rough-in decisions ahead of floor and wall closure. Confirm shower system, fan route, future bar backing and whether this is the only working bath.

Potential work packages include [Protection and removal](tasks.md#protection-and-removal), [Water supply and fixtures](tasks.md#water-supply-and-fixtures), [Waste and drains](tasks.md#waste-and-drains), [Ventilation and comfort](tasks.md#ventilation-and-comfort), [Power and controls](tasks.md#power-and-controls), [Mounting and backing](tasks.md#mounting-and-backing), [Tile and wet assemblies](tasks.md#tile-and-wet-assemblies), [Flooring](tasks.md#flooring), [Cabinets and counters](tasks.md#cabinets-and-counters), [Walls and ceilings](tasks.md#walls-and-ceilings), [Painting and coatings](tasks.md#painting-and-coatings), [Access and safety fittings](tasks.md#access-and-safety-fittings), [Handoff](tasks.md#handoff).

**Walk the bath in build order when the contractor owns the remodel.**

1. Mark what stays. A vanity-and-paint refresh can leave the shower untouched. For an occupied home, agree how long the only toilet may be out, where debris travels and who keeps salvaged pieces.
2. Fix the layout against the selected products. Check the vanity drawer against the trap, toilet clearance against the finished wall, and shower door swing against the vanity. A mismatch can change the purchase before anyone opens a wall.
3. Before closing, settle who handles supply and drain changes, valve depth, fan exhaust to outdoors, lighting and controls, niches and the backing for bars, glass or cabinets. Identify any inspection or test that releases the walls.
4. Assign substrate repair, shower waterproofing and drain connection. Settle tile layout, edges and transitions at the finished elevations. If removal exposes a soft subfloor, show the affected area and agree on the repair and price before continuing.
5. Coordinate finish and fit-off. Confirm when the enclosure can be measured, which accessories are owner-supplied, who resets the toilet and tests fixtures, and who tells the owner when the shower may be used. A vanity installer quoting only their package needs the interfaces touching that package.
