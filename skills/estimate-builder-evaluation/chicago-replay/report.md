# Chicago pricing replay: 100 original requests

**All 100 were rerun twice for pricing, and all 100 received homeowner estimates using the original contractor’s terms. The changes improved agreement with the published prices used in this review. They did not establish better invoice accuracy.** The independent check was mixed: including a conditional exterior-door comparison, average error fell; on the three closer matches alone, it rose.

[Open the complete 100-row scorecard](scorecard.csv). Each row identifies the original request and supplied price terms, before/after planning estimate, local price and source, scope mismatch, signed dollar difference where appropriate, remaining questions, and final homeowner copy. The last column records any post-validation provider options. A blank comparison or “pending” is not a zero-dollar job.

The fixture contains **no completed local invoices for these 100 fictional customers**. Chicago was assigned as a consistent test market, not inferred from the customers. Search included primary contractor menus, booking pages, retailer terms, local homeowner-reported bids and completed-job records. Evidence was captured Nov 13–14, 2026. The scorecard distinguishes city, metro and service-zone conditions in the accompanying notes. Two illustrations of why the quality check matters:

- [GreenPal's Chicago records](https://www.yourgreenpal.com/il/chicago-lawn-care) showed five ten-day bookings at $35, $38, $38, $40 and $42 across three providers, with one or two weeks since the last cut. The original $55 bid was $13 above the highest observed booking. Yard dimensions, precise fence work and blowing were not verified; this is a reason to check the yard, not to cut the bid. The skill's old midpoint was $53.50; its new broad Chicago reference is [$41.44 across 3,294 bookings](https://api.yourgreenpal.com/newsroom/fuel-inflation-lawn-care-prices-high-volume-markets-greenpal). These data come from the same platform. An [additional provider-exclusion check](mowing-protocol.md) did not yield a qualifying fresh provider.
- [Property Refresh's Oakwood Hills job](https://www.property-refresh.com/blog/one-story-gutter-cleaning-oakwood-hills-case-study/) had a $135 invoice of which $120 was cleaning and $15 a new cap. #63 does not request a cap or state the house size. [EasyFix's completed tub job](https://www.easyfixhandymanil.com/caulking-sealing/) cost $275 and also treated mold and resealed a soap dish; #9 is a dry tub-to-tile seam only. A [Chicago-area customer](https://www.homeadvisor.com/rated.JBlanton.130800545.html) reported a $688.50 disposer bid they **declined**; their $500 payment was for drains, not the disposer. None of these amounts is scored as a same-scope invoice.

## Before and after

“Error” below is absolute difference from the comparison point, in dollars per scoped job or visit. For a source band without a published typical price, the retrospective replay uses its midpoint as a convention, not as an observed sale. Signed deltas and the distance of a supplied quote *outside* the published band are separately available in the CSV. Hours, project floors, incomplete totals and incompatible price channels do not enter job-dollar averages.

The first two rows are **retrospective**; nine of the 20 used benchmark pricing directly, and some comparison rows share a provider. The remaining eleven were still examined during development. Their average signed error moved from +$7.45 to +$22.77, so less absolute error does not mean less upward bias in that subset. Neither row proves that future invoices will be closer. Before the fixes, 58 requests had two-sided numbers. Afterward, 45 had two-sided numbers (including conditional branches and an aggregate mowing reference); two others had specialist minimums only. The reduced coverage primarily reflects the skill no longer turning unknown scope into a total. All 100 still have a customer-facing answer; the calculations have a smaller denominator.

The additional check had **six preset profiles**; no eligible fresh five-blind or independent-disposal price was established. We fixed the price predictions before researching new provider pages. It tests six specific branches, including a regional toilet comparator and a two-provider midpoint for blinds, not six new customer requests. No already-used provider card was counted as new. Two branches used different trades from one NW-suburban price book; results are not statistically independent. ## Biggest misses and the changes made

- **Exterior door (#2).** The blind $300–540 range priced a specialist installation as general handyman hours. The revision gives a $650–1,350 local comparison, including [a $1,350 customer-supplied weatherproofed install](https://installscustomerpurchasedmaterials.com/services.php). The original $950 labor offer remains $950. The draft asks who buys flashing and trim consumables. Exact weatherproofing and trim responsibility still need confirmation.
- **Paint and fence (#49, #52).** Six unnamed rooms produced $5,670–11,855; the skill had repeated the highest stand-alone room price six times. It now asks for dimensions and one mobilization; the contractor’s $3,600 bid with paint and a sample visit stands. The fence model assumed cedar and produced $3,518–6,596. On [Legna’s western-suburb card](https://legnairon.com/fence-installation-cost/), 60 net feet plus a four-foot gate is $2,400–2,700 for treated pine or $3,180–3,480 for cedar **before** tear-out. The original agreed $2,850 covers removal. No full-job accuracy score for either.
- **Small jobs (#20, #22, #76, #83, #90).** Use the booked task or shared visit. Two small holes may fit [one $169 visit](https://www.southsidehomeservices.com/drywall-repair-chicago); the old midpoint was $325. [Five ready blinds](https://square.site/book/03PHAR87WHNM0/big-helpers-inc-chicago-il) cost $175 under one seller’s menu. Other sellers can charge differently. The same first-hour fee does not automatically apply to a plumber, report-only inspection or urgent call.
- **Appliance channels (#26, #31, #35, #38, #92).** Compare the checkout charge for an eligible purchase with other retail offers. Compare a standalone installer with other standalone offers. [Abt's $59 washer and $93 electric-dryer services](https://www.abt.com/help/local-installation) total $152 **if eligible**; they do not establish that the contractor’s $500 independent package is $348 overpriced. Likewise the disposer has a $179 retail branch and an [independent plumber's approximate $290 labor price](https://abvplumbing.com/garbage-disposal-installation-and-repair-services/).
- **Pavers (#55–56).** A specialist’s [published floors](https://opportunitylandscaping.us/price-list/) are $1,500 for repair or $5,000 for a new project. The skill now exposes the minimum, rather than multiplying by only 16 or 72 sq ft. The original small-job bids of $260 and $1,440 are not demonstrated errors: a different crew may accept that work without the specialist minimum.
- **Remaining provider disagreement (#7, #10, #83).** After the independent check the final skill now shows multiple service-area and supplier branches. For the assumed TV, these run from a conditional $135 to $317 unpromoted instead of promising the single $207 price. A Chicago-city reseal still needs a city-serving offer. These [last changes](final-corrections.json) are not credited as fresh accuracy gains on the same observations.

For the largest remaining *contractor* differences, sort the CSV by absolute distance outside the local band: #2 ($400 below a conditional $1,350 offer), #92 ($348 above a retail-only branch), #27 ($166 above a standard cabinet cut), and #60 ($159 above a small-sod unit-price extension). These are review prompts with materially different unresolved conditions, not customer price changes. Other misses remain: e.g. #25’s updated visit midpoint is $45.50 over the $120 first-hour branch and #27 still has a $73.50 planning midpoint gap. Confirm actual billed time, cabinet modifications and standalone minimums before making adjustments.

## Reproduction and limits

The original requests and contractor terms live in [baseline.jsonl](../baseline.jsonl). The runners received the redacted customer request and follow-up scope together (this was a **pricing** replay, not a repeat of the initial interview test); three batches of 33/33/34 used the [pre-fix skill](pre-fix-skill.zip), without web targets. Its ZIP SHA-256 was 5fe76ebb97f493a84a6e11424fffb9ccca719ca743e1a4eee63194d43f1d1616. Forecasts were saved before local comparison research. Some evaluators had prior exposure to the fictional suite; this was not a first-ever double-blind trial. After the research, the skill was changed and all planning cases were replayed. The original contractor figures were separately restored for customer drafts. Final editorial checks preserved open issues such as the exterior-door flashing supplier, mantel clearance and settled trench backfill. The source evidence files retain raw classifications; score.py applies the final stricter adjudication, including removing the soap-dish job. All data and computations run locally with Python’s standard library. Published cards will change. We did not contact providers, book jobs or verify private receipts.
