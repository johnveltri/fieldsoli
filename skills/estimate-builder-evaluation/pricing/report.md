# U.S. residential pricing evaluation

**Completed-job pricing:** [Blind multi-city estimates compared with itemized homeowner payments](../paid-handyman-jobs/report.md).

**The tested pricing methods improved overall accuracy on comparable public targets.** The previous skill returned a scope without a price when given no contractor figure. It had no independent price forecast to score in those cases. The original 100 test prices were fictional contractor inputs or unpriced scopes. Repeating one correctly measures price preservation; it does not establish market accuracy. We separately measured the old examples where sufficiently comparable prices could be found. The most direct comparison with the old examples reduced error by **10.7%** on the same fifteen targets. Dishwashers actually worsened from $25.41 to $28.57 MAE, while microwave-plus-haul improved from $47.58 to $36.28. The three broad-project methods are evaluated against owner accounts, and reported spend can include owner purchases. For the broadest held-out cohort, the regional method was within 25% only **21.62%** of the time. Its empirical p10–p90 range covered **79.74%** of outcomes. Historical budgets need measured local inputs before they can stand behind a bid.

## What the skill now does

For a budget request the skill checks scope, ZIP/metro, date, quantities, who supplies what and stage of completion. It selects local comparable offers or builds the line from labor, product selection, setup, haul, taxes and permit/subcontract charges as applicable. The work-package directory routes all 42 indexed task families and the project/appliance maps. The skill includes 159 historical project/geography bands and 1,347 employee wage observations by occupation and area. Employee wages are an input into estimating contractor costs. The crew's experience can affect both rate and hours; directly observed role-specific rates and contractor production data take precedence over guessed seniority multipliers.

The Voorhees replay produced a $265 dishwasher planning comparison for the requested install and haul. When the contractor expressly quoted $260 the skill kept $260. The Chicago kitchen replay narrowed a 210 sq ft full replacement using a dated, scoped package; after a correction to retained cabinets and owner-paid countertops, it dropped the full-remodel figure. An ADU with unknown location and dimensions remained unpriced pending scope. ## Scope of the findings

These are separate comparisons, not one universal percentage or evidence of current final-invoice accuracy across all 100 scenarios. The test sets mix historical reported costs, provider offers and bids, with dates and units documented separately. Material grade, location, scope and supplier frequently vary together. Several sources describe contractual minimums, only some include materials, and taxable treatment can differ. No experiment here isolates a causal effect of years in trade or validates a universal adjustment for complexity. For uncommon jobs, custom structures, unknown site conditions or finishes with no matching local evidence, the skill gives the supported pieces and identifies the pending quote or measurement.
