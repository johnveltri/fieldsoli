# Remodel benchmark prediction protocol

Frozen before fetching the target regional tables. Predict 2025 JLC/Zonda modeled job cost per region for five defined prototypes: midrange minor kitchen, midrange and upscale major kitchen, midrange and upscale bath remodel. Regions fixed as New England, Mountain, South Atlantic and East North Central. Search previews included other regions and national figures; none of these twenty target values were used to select or tune a factor.

Predictor A uses the US 2025 national job cost for the identical prototype. Predictor B multiplies that cost by the matching region's 2024 job cost divided by the 2024 US job cost for the identical prototype. No fitting, error-driven retuning, exclusions for large misses, inflation multiplier, or rounding until display. Missing or incompatible prototype: report missing. Compare equal-cell mean absolute percentage error, median absolute percentage error, maximum miss, dollar MAE, and share within 10%. Show every row. Prior skill supplies no numeric benchmark. These correlated publisher estimates test forecasting regional table entries, not independent homeowners' prices; national reports can include some of the target markets in their aggregate.

## Independent household outcome test

Before downloading project-level 2023 AHS data, freeze this separate test on positive reported kitchen and bath remodel costs. Split by household, SHA-256 of the CONTROL identifier stripped of CSV quotes, first eight hex digits modulo 5 equals zero for test, otherwise train. Median baseline pools kitchens and baths and both DIY statuses. Improved method uses a separate median per project type and DIY status. Calculate median and empirical 10th–90th percentile bounds from training only, unweighted. Report holdout MAE, median absolute percentage error, percent within 25%, interval coverage and total count by approach. This tests point and interval behavior for coarse descriptions on actual reported expenditure. No outlier trimming or cost-based scope labels. Aggregate national survey medians encountered during source discovery are not inputs. Cluster splitting prevents two projects from the same household leaking across splits.
