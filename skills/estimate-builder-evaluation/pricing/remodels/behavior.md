# Two-turn Chicago kitchen planning budget

Manual interaction using the skill entrypoint, pricing guide, kitchen scope playbook and major-remodel comparisons. Source prices checked November 2026. Inputs and outputs follow verbatim.

## First contractor input

Homeowner wants a planning budget for a 210-sq-ft kitchen, all cabinets and appliances replaced, Chicago. Selections still open. No quoted price. Show me evidence and what would narrow it.

**Contractor review**

For a same-layout kitchen, the closest local example I found is **$50,000–$58,000**. A Chicago remodeler publishes that range for a 200–240 sq ft demolition and rebuild with semi-custom cabinets, stone counters, an appliance package, flooring and painted finishes. That matches your room size, but several items in the example still need the owner’s decision. As a separate check, US Houzz respondents who replaced all cabinets and appliances in kitchens of at least 200 sq ft reported a **$55,000 median** in 2025. The national 90th percentile was $150,000; it describes high spending across those respondents. [Chicago package details](https://kitchenremodeling-chicago.com/kitchen-remodel-cost/mid-range/) · [Houzz size and scope breakdown](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/11-things-to-know-about-home-renovations-today-stsetivw-vs~185079173)

Will the sink, range and walls stay put? And do the owners want that whole package, including counters and floors? A measured cabinet plan, a supplier quote for their chosen cabinet line and an appliance model list will narrow the largest selections. Confirm counter area and the installation responsibilities before quoting.

## Second contractor input

Correction: cabinet boxes and appliances stay, owner pays counter fabricator separately. Now give me a revised budget.

## Second response

**Contractor review**

The revised cabinet budget depends on what happens to the fronts. For painting existing fronts, two Chicago providers publish 2026 planning rates of **$100–$250** and **$150–$250 per door or drawer front**. For refacing the retained boxes with new fronts, Fulton lists **$250–$450 per front**. Count each door and drawer front once. Check whether the matching box faces, exposed ends, hinges and pulls are included before extending either rate. [Transform & Restore painting](https://transformandrestorepaint.com/blog/kitchen-cabinet-painting-cost-chicago/) · [Fulton Revivals painting and refacing](https://fultonrevivals.com/faq/)

Does the homeowner want paint, replacement fronts, or the existing cabinet finish left alone? How many fronts and exposed ends are there? Also confirm who removes the old top, mounts the sink and reconnects the plumbing. Paying the fabricator directly settles who pays for that package, but its written scope determines which tasks remain yours.

**Planning estimate — contractor confirmation pending**

Your cabinet boxes and appliances stay, and you’ll pay your countertop fabricator directly. For Chicago in November 2026, if you choose to update the cabinets, allow **$100–$250 per existing front for painting**, or **$250–$450 per front for refacing**. Choose one approach. If the cabinet finish stays as it is, neither allowance applies.

The cabinet subtotal is pending your choice and the front count. Countertop fabrication and appliance purchases are outside my budget. I’ll price countertop removal or sink reconnection only if we confirm those are my tasks.

## Behavior observed

The first turn gave a conditional local package alongside a clearly identified national median. The corrected homeowner copy removed the earlier major-remodel total, kept owner-paid fabrication outside the contractor’s work, and gave locally sourced alternative cabinet unit budgets. It carried no assumed front count, finish choice, or fabricated total. This tests one scope correction; no contractor quote or paid outcome was available for an accuracy score.
