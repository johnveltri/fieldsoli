# Deck-board retail transfer protocol

Frozen before searching either seller for the comparison. Test a comparable deck-material purchase separate from installed-package offers. Use one seller for training and one independent seller as a holdout. The two materials are Trex Enhance Basics (Clam Shell or Saddle) and Enhance Naturals (Foggy Wharf or Rocky Harbor), grooved nominal 1x6, 16-foot standard straight boards. Search for independent US lumber dealers listing actual per-board prices for these variants, or per-linear-foot prices with 16-foot availability. Prioritize dealer-owned sites with visible, unambiguous prices and confirm the SKU or named board, length and edge. Exclude clearance/sale, bulk or member-only prices, freight, stocked cosmetic seconds, online marketplaces, add-to-cart-only prices and product pages showing a range without a selectable price. First fully eligible seller in search-result order is training; next is holdout. At each named grade use price from the training seller as the grade-aware prediction; the baseline is the mean of its two prices for either board. Score MAE per 16-foot board and report signed error and vendor/variant counts. Different regions are acceptable but disclose them; don't pool with installation and mowing. Stop after three search rounds if no eligible pair.
