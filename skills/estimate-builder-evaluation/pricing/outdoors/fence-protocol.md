# Fence bid comparison protocol

Frozen before opening the Toms River, NJ 2022 negotiated bid tabulation. Its search snippet revealed two optional lines (post spacing and a 3-foot gate). Exclude both and all other optional lines. Read only the first base-installed fence family with at least two height variations and at least two distinct numerical bidder columns. Use the first bidder's rate for each height as the policy prediction and their median across the selected heights as the constant baseline. Predict every remaining bidder's corresponding per-linear-foot rate. Exclude blanks or nonnumeric bids pairwise; no region or vendor calibration. Record every included row and signed/absolute error, report MAE and provider/project counts. If families do not have the same material, fence type and unit, skip them. This is transfer between competing bidders on a single procurement: no claim about acceptance, retail residential markup or eventual payment.
