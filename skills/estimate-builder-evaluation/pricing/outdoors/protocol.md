# Outdoor validation protocol

Frozen before looking up prices. Compare advertised mowing offers at independent providers in different markets. For each provider, use first mowing-only residential recurring table with at least two explicit lawn-area bands. Select ordinary mowing at the provider's most frequent normal interval. Require dollar prices per visit and express sizes in square feet (43,560 sq ft/acre). Record published ranges or starting rates as such. For numeric scoring use the lower bound for all offers if the vendor quotes a range or minimum. No full-season packages, one-time cleanup, annualized rates, or extrapolation beyond coverage.

Take the first two independent eligible providers returned by search in discovery order. Designate the first as training, second as held-out. At each of the held-out provider's finite upper area limits in both providers' supported ranges, predict its per-visit offer from the training provider's band for that size. Baseline is the arithmetic median of the training table's finite-band prices for every size. Score mean absolute error in dollars, report case count, absolute error per row and bias. Also report the observed held-out offer range and whether tiering won. This tests transfer between vendors and climates, not predictive accuracy on paid jobs. No post-hoc calibration. Keep the vendors' service inclusions visible.

For paving, fencing, decking, sod or landscape supplies, preserve actual producer/vendor grade, units, region, scope, advertised status and source date separately. No estimate of installation from retail price alone. Use an installed provider offer when verifiable. State the relevant unmeasured dimensions rather than create a national numerical model for these families.
