# Service-price audit

Prices observed November 13, 2026, in USD. Run `python3 evaluate.py` in this directory. The script reads the dated provider extracts in `cards.json` and the separately collected `challenge.csv`. It recreates both holdout and proxy itemized results, plus the prospective challenge. The JSON contains the source URL, scope, price type, date and city for every initial observation. The eligible card sample was purposive across US service companies and dealers. It does not estimate a national distribution or a local market multiplier.

## Holdout and prospective results

The initial [protocol](protocol.md) was written before opening the price search. For each point, withhold its entire provider. Compare the remaining median in the same service to a pooled median across the remaining project fees. Compare hourly rows only with other hourly rows. One basic appliance installer includes haul in its posted standard rate; others charge separately. Those base scopes, retail channels, inclusions, provider ages and local markets differ. Combining them creates comparability error. Card observation date is separate from effective date. Brown identifies January 1, 2024; Winchester identifies April 21, 2026. Most others provide no price-effective date. Do not infer an effective date from site copyright.

The [prospective freeze](prospective.md) logged numeric predictions before searching new providers. On four eligible ordinary price lines, MAE was $32.75, with three within 20%. The sole miss was $132 versus $200 for a standard 55-inch Houston TV. In Olympia estimates were $195/$225 dishwasher, $165/$190 over-range/hood (shared price), and $167/$175 disposal. The Seattle provider’s owner-supplied disposal description says $145 and its checkout heading says $165; abstain on that fifth basic line. Its dishwasher package includes local pickup as well as installation and haul. Scored separately as a proxy, $235 versus $225, absolute error $10. No separate Seattle install-only amount is published. Two Washington providers and one Texas provider met the selection rule; the test did not reach a New England provider. Source links are included per row in the [challenge input](challenge.csv). The independent Houston price card later checked for context lists $65 for 55 inches, underlining within-market variation; this later card was outside the one-provider challenge. ## Original case prices

The baseline’s figures came from fictional contractors. For cases 26 and 31 compare the $260 and $240 supplied prices with standard installation plus published one-piece haul. For case 38 compare the $180 swap with standard disposal installation. Omit the provider which automatically includes disposal haul. Scope uncertainty remains around flange, testing, supplies and sales channel. These 15 paired comparisons give MAE $31.73 and 10/15 within 20%. Misses: case 26 Tucker $260/$195; case 31 Grand $240/$178, Tucker $240/$170, Voorhees $240/$179; case 38 Brown $180/$120. All 15 targets and predictions are in `fictional_proxies.csv`. This describes historical case inputs, not pricing skill learned by the model. A paid or local matched price would be needed to judge a contractor's actual estimate. [Original appliance cases](../../cases-026-050.md)

Additional comparison after the prospective test: case 10’s $220 over-mantel TV and raceway has no stated screen size or wall material. For the specific scenario of a 55-inch screen, drywall above a fireplace, supplied bracket and a fireplace surface cover in Houston, the card is $200 + $50 + $125 = $375. The proxy misses by $155 (41.3%). It cannot diagnose an error in the fictional contractor’s quote; those installation particulars and market were unspecified. This scenario is outside the 15-comparison score. [Provider](https://www.themountingman.com/houston/) · [Original TV case](../../cases-001-025.md)

## Paid evidence and exclusions

A customer’s first-person account of a completed installation reports $175 for the dishwasher installation and $20 haul-away in 2023. Compared with the $235 with-haul card median, the $195 reported total differs by $40 (20.5%). No receipt, service area, materials breakdown or independent verification accompanied the accessible account. Treat this as self-reported, with a three-year date gap. The source was accessible in indexed text, but direct retrieval failed. Another first-person complaint reports paying $190 for installation and $50 for haul but also describes a subsequent plumber’s quote; a fee paid at order time is insufficient proof of the final charge for completed work. Neither enters the card validation score. [Customer account](https://www.reddit.com/r/Appliances/comments/16ffqf5/) · [BBB customer complaint](https://www.bbb.org/us/ga/atlanta/profile/building-materials/the-home-depot-0443-4588/complaints?page=924)

Gothenburg, Nebraska school district check 5000, January 31, 2026, paid $877.29 for a dishwasher installation (invoice 7646361-1, food-service account). Applying the residential $195 prediction would understate that payment by $682.29. The equipment, commercial labor and parts are unspecified. A separate 2019 elementary-school journal records a $1,460.28 installation expense and a separate commercial dishwasher equipment purchase. These are documented disbursements and illustrate why a residential method must abstain on institutional work. A 2020 homeowner damage claim listed $160 installation as a replacement cost estimate; payment of that line was not established. We found no publicly documented paid US residential invoice matching the test.
