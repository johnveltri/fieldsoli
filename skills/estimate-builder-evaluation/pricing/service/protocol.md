# Provider-holdout protocol

Frozen before the first price search on November 13, 2026. Seek independent US handyman or appliance installer rate cards posting basic disposal swap, dishwasher install, over-range microwave install, standard stud-wall TV mount, or a handyman hourly rate. Include every eligible service per selected provider once, taking the ordinary first-unit, existing-services, standard size/configuration, single-technician price. For tiered TV use 55 inches; for hourly use the one-worker regular hourly figure, recording minimums separately. For ambiguous bands, show limitations; do not score ranges or an amount labeled “starting at” as if it were a fixed bill. Report separately if using clearly labeled published floors. Exclude retail hardware bundles and membership discounts. Seek at least two providers per tested category; sparse categories abstain.

Leave one entire provider out at a time. Predict a held-out rate using the median other-provider rate for that service and same rate type (fixed or floor). Compare against median across all other-provider project rates of same rate type, never pooling hourly with per-project. Report dollars of absolute error, percent within ±20% of the target, all misses and abstentions. Price card endpoints are evidence of advertised rates; a receipt is needed to score actual realized cost. Document geography, service inclusions and limitations. Historical fictional case 26 dishwasher $260 includes haul, 31 microwave $240 includes haul, 38 disposal $180 retains old unit, 10 TV $220 includes mantel and raceway. Use direct comparison only when coverage is comparable; otherwise label partial comparisons as proxies or omit. Do not tune thresholds based on results.
