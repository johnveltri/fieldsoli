Help improve FieldSoli?

FieldSoli uses optional product analytics to understand reliability and improve features. Analytics stay off unless you tap Allow analytics below.

If enabled, we may collect:

  • App interactions (screens and actions you take)
  • Device platform and app version
  • Apple Ads campaign information, linked to your app activity, to measure which ads are useful
  • Coarse error categories (not raw error text)

We do not sell your data. You can change this anytime in Privacy Choices. See our Privacy Policy for details.
