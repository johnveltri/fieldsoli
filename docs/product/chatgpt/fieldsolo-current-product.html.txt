    <section id="purpose">
      <h2>Purpose, audience, and boundaries</h2>
      <div class="grid">
        <div class="panel">
          <h3>Purpose</h3>
          <p>FieldSoli helps an individual skilled-trade operator rapidly capture job activity, recall what happened, complete the job record, and understand earnings without maintaining a heavyweight field-service system.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="panel">
          <h3>Primary audience</h3>
          <p>Solo tradespeople and independent service operators who need a reliable personal record of jobs, time, notes, materials, payment state, and profitability.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="panel">
          <h3>Current non-goals</h3>
          <ul>
            <li>No quoting, estimates, invoices, payment processing, dispatch, scheduling, routing, team management, or customer portal.</li>
            <li>No promise of offline-first synchronization.</li>
            <li>No claim that exploring Figma experiments are available in the app.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section id="navigation" data-status="shipped">
      <h2>Navigation and screen hierarchy</h2>
      <ol>
        <li>Anonymous users see <a href="#screen-sign-in">Sign in / Sign up</a>.</li>
        <li>Authenticated users must pass the <a href="#state-legal-reacceptance">legal-version gate</a> and, when unresolved, the <a href="#state-analytics-consent">analytics choice</a>.</li>
        <li>The persistent main shell contains <a href="#screen-home">Home</a>, <a href="#screen-jobs">Jobs</a>, and <a href="#screen-earnings">Earnings</a>.</li>
        <li><a href="#screen-job-detail">Job Detail</a> and <a href="#state-inbox-notes">Inbox</a> cover the shell but preserve the selected destination tab.</li>
        <li><a href="#screen-profile">Profile</a> stacks over Home; <a href="#state-help">Help</a> and <a href="#state-privacy">Privacy</a> are secondary Profile screens.</li>
        <li>An active live session is represented globally by <a href="#state-live-session">Live Session</a> UI.</li>
      </ol>
    </section>

      <h2>Entities, statuses, and business rules</h2>
      <table>
        <thead><tr><th>Entity</th><th>Status / rule</th><th>Meaning</th><th>Relationships</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody>
          <tr data-entity="job" data-rule-id="job-work-status"><td>Job</td><td>notStarted, inProgress, completed, onHold, cancelled; derived paid</td><td>Work status describes operational progress. Paid is presented when payment is paid.</td><td>Owns sessions, notes, and materials.</td></tr>
          <tr data-entity="job" data-rule-id="job-payment-status"><td>Job payment</td><td>pending or paid</td><td>Completed jobs with null/pending payment appear in pending-payment attention and Open.</td><td>Separate from work status.</td></tr>
          <tr data-entity="job" data-rule-id="job-financial-completeness"><td>Job completeness</td><td>Derived</td><td>Missing description, revenue, materials/no-materials confirmation, or sessions produces incomplete attention.</td><td>Drives Home and Jobs Open grouping and gates Paid and earnings snapshots.</td></tr>
          <tr data-entity="session" data-rule-id="session-state"><td>Session</td><td>in progress or ended</td><td><code>endedAt=null</code> means active/in progress; ended sessions have duration and display labels.</td><td>Belongs to one job; may own note/material attachments.</td></tr>
          <tr data-entity="note" data-rule-id="note-parent"><td>Note</td><td>Inbox, job-scoped, or session-scoped</td><td>Unassigned note has no job; assignment attaches it to a job. Delete is soft-delete.</td><td>Optional job/session parent.</td></tr>

    <section id="privacy-and-safety" data-status="shipped">
      <h2>Authentication, consent, privacy, and destructive actions</h2>
      <div class="grid">
        <div class="panel"><h3>Authentication</h3><p>Supabase email/password authentication controls access. Local migration preserves prior Fieldbook auth storage only when the fieldsolo.* auth key does not already exist. Every new authenticated session enters on Home rather than restoring the screen or overlay that was open before sign-out.</p></div>
        <div class="panel"><h3>Legal acceptance</h3><p>Signup and reacceptance persist versioned Privacy/Terms acceptance. A current server record is authoritative; a current user/version-specific cache can bridge temporary server failure.</p></div>
        <div class="panel"><h3>Analytics</h3><p>Optional analytics do not activate before consent. Granting activates identify/events; withdrawal clears the persisted anonymous identifier and stops events.</p></div>
        <div class="panel"><h3>Permanent account deletion</h3><p class="danger">Profile requires the phrase <code>delete account</code>, then a system confirmation. The delete-account Edge Function permanently removes the account and associated data.</p></div>
        <div class="panel"><h3>Record deletion</h3><p>Job, session, note, and material deletion require explicit user action. Notes and materials use soft deletion; the active live-session delete path deletes the session record.</p></div>
        <div class="panel"><h3>Release constraints</h3><p>Production validation rejects a local backend and rich analytics debugging. Analytics provider configuration does not override the consent gate.</p></div>
      </div>
    </section>
