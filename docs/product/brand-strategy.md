# Brand Essence

Light, Generous, Empowered, Soli

# External Response

“No crew, no office staff, just me, so most contractor software is overkill. FieldSoli is quick, doesn’t lock every useful feature behind a subscription, and after seeing that a few similar jobs kept taking longer than I expected, I finally raised what I charge for them.”

# Brand Promise

FieldSoli believes working solo should be a strength, not a disadvantage. We give solo residential handymen the advantages of a larger business without the cost or complexity, so they can focus on the work, price with confidence, and benefit from a community of fellow soli.

# Brand Pillars (Points of Difference)

### Exceptional value for independents

Big-business leverage at a price that makes sense for a one-person business.

### Field-speed simplicity

Spend as little time administering the job as possible. Capture now, organize later, automate wherever we can.

### Consumer-grade experience

Contractor software can be clear, modern, beautiful and genuinely pleasant to use.

### Learn from every job

Turn each real outcome into timely guidance that helps you price and choose the next job better.

### Stronger together

Independent doesn’t mean alone. Soli gain knowledge, opportunities and leverage from one another while remaining independent.

# Brand Attributes (Points of Parity)

### Basic job records

Job details including work status/state, job history, and a date associated to the work / know what’s coming.

### Customer records

Customer name, email/phone contact info, Service address and Customer job history

### Job documentation

Notes, Photos/files, Materials and other job costs

### Estimates / quotes

Create and send a basic estimate/quote. Includes Line items, scope, price and acceptance status

### Invoices & receipts

Turn completed work into an invoice, send/share it, provide proof of payment

### Payments

Accept digital payment, Record offline payment, Track paid / unpaid / partial / balance due

### Basic per-job financial state

Amount quoted/invoiced, Costs recorded against the job, Amount paid, Balance outstanding (Not: sophisticated interpretation of those numbers.)

### Export your business records

Basic CSV/data export is enough to satisfy parity, Customers, jobs and financial records should be portable
