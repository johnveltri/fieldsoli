## Feature graphic

[06-feature-graphic.png](06-feature-graphic.png) is the separate **1024 × 500** Google Play feature graphic, in RGB PNG without transparency. It uses the same edge-to-edge frame and exact approved copy:

> Unlimited invoices & estimates
>
> Simple time & expense tracking for contractors.

The [feature-graphic compositor](../../creative-assets/authoring/compose-android-feature-graphic.py) reuses the existing invoice plate and campaign artwork. The output was visually reviewed and its dimensions verified. No upload or publication was performed.

These are frame adaptations of the supplied app screenshots, not fresh Android device captures. The original iOS status bars are cropped out; the remaining app content is uniformly scaled, with no invented Android controls. The original iOS campaign PNGs are unchanged.

[Google Play specifications](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151): these files are 9:16, RGB PNG without transparency, 1080 × 1920, and below the 3840-pixel maximum. They satisfy the maximum 2:1 long-to-short dimension constraint.

The [compositor](../../creative-assets/authoring/compose-android-screenshots.py) preserves approved lettering and original native UI pixels, uses the retained campaign backdrop, and reapplies the actual exported data excerpt. Source hashes, crop coordinates, and native-pixel checks are recorded in [validation.json](validation.json). Revisions overwrite these filenames. Full-set visual review and the export-button check were completed; these files have not been uploaded or published.
