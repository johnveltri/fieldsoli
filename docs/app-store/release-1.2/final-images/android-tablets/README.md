# FieldSoli 1.2 landscape tablet artwork

Open [the review gallery](review.html) to inspect both device sets. Click an image to open its full-resolution PNG.

Five campaign images per device, **2560 × 1600**, landscape RGB PNG without transparency. Fresh native Android captures are framed consistently with a minimal black tablet border. There is no iPhone hardware, Dynamic Island, or added FieldSoli wordmark. Source UI is uniformly scaled; controls, amounts, and buttons are not redrawn or stretched.

Image 01 follows the approved invoice v3 direction: job and charges are prominent; a separate partial business/customer crop overlaps the upper-right edge. Image 02 shows individual materials and costs, with a separate original visit card where needed. Image 04 includes the existing real export excerpt as a separate card and keeps the native request button visible. Image 05 shows job rankings by net/hour, with a separate least-profitable-job excerpt on Nexus; Pixel shows both ranking sections directly.

These are **review candidates**, not a release-binary QA verdict or published store changes. The demo fixture was reset with explicit owner approval. Amounts, dates, visit duration, and document numbers reflect the live fixture during capture and can differ from the older phone campaign. The invoice and estimate previews were not sent to anyone.
